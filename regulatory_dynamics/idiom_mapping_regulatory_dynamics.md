## Overview

Across cultures, people describe internal state transitions, autonomic 
shifts, cognitive narrowing, and social co-regulation using stable 
idioms. These idioms are not poetic abstractions — they are behavioral 
surface-expressions of the same mechanisms formalized in the Unified 
Model's contracts.

This document maps each idiom class to the specific contracts and 
crossover points that generate the underlying dynamics. Each mapping 
includes verbatim lines from the relevant contracts to make 
verification direct.

## Why This Document Exists

Every idiom in this document is already in common use. You have said 
most of them. The people around you have said most of them. They exist 
in every language because the mechanisms they describe are structural — 
not cultural, not metaphorical, not poetic.

The framework did not discover these phenomena. It discovered the 
mechanism behind phenomena that folk language had already correctly 
identified and named. "Tunnel vision" is not a colorful way of saying 
someone is stubborn. It is a precise mechanical description of a 
narrowed sampling distribution. "Can't catch your breath" is not 
sympathy language. It is an accurate readout of incomplete exhalation 
and its downstream cognitive consequence. "Sleeping on it" is not folk 
wisdom. It is a description of SWS pruning and REM prior updating 
happening on a specific overnight timescale.

The idioms are the signal. They persist across cultures and centuries 
because the nervous system that generates them is the same nervous 
system the contracts describe. What was missing was not the 
observation — it was the mechanism. This document provides it.
