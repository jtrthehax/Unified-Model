## What This Document Is

This is not a trait list. It is not a clinical profile. It is a description of a single regulatory architecture — high precision gain driven by elevated anterior insula Glx — expressing across two attractor states that produce radically different outputs from identical substrate.

The same person. The same gain floor. Attractor A: accelerated cross-domain synthesis, hyperfocus, the thought of progress as fuel. Attractor B: acute interoceptive prior capture, lock-in rumination, FND.

Understanding which attractor the system is in explains everything. Understanding only the traits explains nothing.

---

## The Unifying Parameter

The architecture rests on a single substrate variable: elevated anterior insula Glx [^1].

This sets the precision gain floor — the amplitude at which prediction errors fire and at which their resolution registers. Everything else is downstream of this one parameter:

- High-amplitude prediction errors → strong pull toward resolution
- High-amplitude resolution events → high-contrast GABAergic suppression against the Glx background → the phenomenological "click" of insight is loud
- Anticipatory D2 signal weighted proportionally to the gain floor → the *thought* of making progress fires as strongly as progress itself
- GABA suppression working against a high-Glx background → when GABA availability drops, errors don't close cleanly → rumination and lock-in are proportionally larger in magnitude

The same parameter. The same direction. The difference is which attractor the system is currently operating in and whether GABA availability is sufficient to close what the Glx floor opens.

## The Compound Architecture

Before describing the attractors, the hardware preconditions need to be named — because the synthesis loop only runs at full amplitude when all of them are present simultaneously.

**The wide-window ceiling** — unusually high CO₂ tolerance raises the bracing threshold so exhale amplitude reaches full depth, opening the prediction window to maximum width. Without the hardware ceiling, the synthesis loop has a low amplitude cap that prevents cross-domain manifold construction.

**The ADHD anti-rigidity function** — the 2-stroke engine's sampling phase never fully terminates. This is not distractibility. It is the search radius remaining large while precision is running. Without it, the autism precision system locks deeply into one manifold and cannot bridge to the next. With it, deep precision and wide exploration run simultaneously — the compound that produces cross-domain synthesis rather than deep single-domain refinement.

**The autism precision system** — high-gain prediction error processing drives rapid structural updating of the manifold. Each error propagates across the right-hemisphere global manifold because the geometry holds multiple domains in the same space. This is not special interest intensity. It is the correction from a single error propagating structurally rather than staying local.

**Elevated anterior insula Glx** — the gain floor that makes all of the above run at high amplitude in both directions.

None of these produces the synthesis loop alone. Together they produce what the framework calls the hyper-adaptive compound: deep cross-domain synthesis without manifold calcification, running at high velocity, sustained by anticipatory reward rather than completed reward.

## Attractor A — Synthesis Mode

**Operating conditions:**
- Prediction window wide and intact
- Non-threat-dominant prior
- GABA availability sufficient to close errors cleanly
- Mechanical anchor intact (exhale amplitude, parasympathetic baseline)

**What the cycle looks like:**

1. Wide window + non-threat prior → high-gain error processing drives manifold construction
2. Each resolved error propagates structurally across the RH global manifold → cross-domain connections form
3. Resolution registers as high-contrast GABA suppression event → the click of insight is high-amplitude
4. Anticipatory D2 signal fires on predicted completion → the *thought* of making progress generates enough motivational energy to initiate the next cycle before the current one fully resolves
5. ADHD anti-rigidity keeps the search radius expanding while precision runs → next error is drawn from a wider domain than the one just resolved
6. Prior is richer after each cycle → next error is more precisely targeted → resolution is faster
7. Loop compounds

**The subjective texture of Attractor A:**

Being pulled forward rather than pushing. The problem wants to be solved. Hours pass without the sense of time passing. The connections feel obvious in retrospect — not because they were easy, but because the manifold built them into the geometry so that the next step was already visible before it was taken. The nap that follows is not rest from the work. It is the glymphatic reset that allows the same loop to run again at full amplitude the next day, with the previous cycle's manifold gains preserved [^2].

**Why it doesn't look impressive from outside:**

The external observer sees: someone working intensely on something for hours. They don't see the compound mechanism. They see a trait. The synthesis acceleration, the cross-domain bridges, the fact that the prior is being rebuilt structurally rather than locally — none of that is visible in behavior. What's visible is hyperfocus, which the silo view labels as a symptom rather than as the synthesis loop running correctly.

## The Transition Mechanism

The tipping point from Attractor A to Attractor B is specific. It is not general stress. It is not difficulty. It is a particular combination:

**Sustained high-precision engagement with an unresolvable problem.**

What "unresolvable" means mechanistically: the prediction error fires at high amplitude (Glx floor), drives processing, but the prior update that would allow GABA suppression cannot complete — because the information needed to close the error is not available, the problem is genuinely open, or the framing is incorrect and no amount of processing resolves it under the current prior.

The sequence:

1. High-gain error fires and does not resolve cleanly
2. GABA suppression attempts but cannot fully inhibit against the Glx-driven amplitude
3. Error persists at sub-threshold activation — not strong enough to drive new synthesis, not suppressed enough to close
4. Sustained open error becomes the dominant input to the descending interoceptive prediction system
5. Prior begins updating toward alert state: "this problem is unresolved, the body should be in urgency"
6. Active inference: body generates physiological correlates of urgency without ascending signal requiring it
7. Sympathetic tone rises
8. Elevated sympathetic tone reduces GABA availability
9. Reduced GABA means the original error suppresses even less cleanly
10. Prior updates further toward alert state
11. Loop closes and tightens

**The speed differential:** Chronic prior drift in FND develops over months to years. High-gain acute prior capture can complete within a single session — hours, not years — because the gain floor sets the speed. Same mechanism. Same attractor. Different timescale.

## The Threat-Narrow Intermediate State

The transition mechanism describes a trajectory — the system moving from Attractor A toward Attractor B under a specific acute trigger. But there is a third operating state that is neither in motion nor in full lock-in. It is parked. And it is where most high-gain profiles in chronically elevated sympathetic environments spend most of their time.

The Threat-Narrow Intermediate state is what the architecture looks like when GABA is partially available but asymmetrically deployed. The prediction window is narrow and stable — not collapsing further, not reopening. The prior is threat-dominant but not fully captured by an acute unresolvable error. Sympathetic tone is elevated enough to suppress GABA availability below the threshold required for corrections to close, but not so depleted that the system tips into somatic urgency generation. The system is functional. It is also systematically misconfigured.

The error-detection mechanism is still running at full gain. High anterior insula Glx means prediction errors fire at high amplitude regardless of attractor state — the gain floor doesn't drop because the window is narrow. What changes is what the errors fire on. With a threat-dominant prior, the high-gain system is no longer finding cross-domain connections and synthesis opportunities. It is finding flaws, inconsistencies, and wrongs — at the same amplitude, with the same velocity, with the same strong pull toward resolution that characterizes Attractor A.

This is the nit-picking mechanism precisely specified. It is not a personality trait. It is the error-detection system operating correctly on a threat-biased sampling distribution. Every imperfection registers as a high-amplitude prediction error. The pull toward resolution is intact and strong. But resolution in a threat-dominant prior means confirming the threat, not updating away from it — and the errors that confirm threat close cleanly because they don't require the window to widen. The errors that would correct the prior do not close, because processing a correction requires holding the current prior and the challenger simultaneously in the prediction window, and a narrow window cannot do that. The brace fires exactly when the window needs to be widest.

The result is a system that detects everything wrong at high precision and cannot update on any of it. The errors accumulate without resolving. Each unresolved correction adds to the unresolved error residual, which elevates sympathetic tone slightly further, which reduces GABA availability slightly further, which makes the next correction slightly harder to close. The prior does not drift toward threat through a single acute event. It consolidates slowly, mechanically, one unclosed correction at a time.

From outside, the behavioral signature reads as a personality configuration: hypercritical, resistant to feedback, simultaneously negative and incurious. The framework reads it as the correct mechanical output of a high-gain precision system operating on a threat-dominant prior without the window width to process updates. Not a character flaw. Not learned helplessness. The arithmetic consequence of partial GABA availability creating selective closure — where selective means threat-confirming errors close and corrections don't.

The population-level implication follows directly. Environments that chronically elevate sympathetic tone — through engagement-optimized media, threat-salient information environments, high-load institutional compliance architecture — are not merely making people anxious [^4]. They are pharmacologically shifting the population toward this intermediate state by depleting the GABA availability that would allow prediction errors to close as synthesis rather than as threat confirmation. The error-detection capacity remains. The correction capacity is suppressed. The population becomes increasingly proficient at identifying what is wrong and increasingly unable to update toward what would be better. The architecture is the same across every individual in that environment. The attractor the environment selects determines everything downstream.

The exit from the intermediate state is the same substrate-first sequence as the exit from full lock-in, but the threshold is lower. Because GABA is partially available rather than depleted, amplitude restoration does not need to work against a fully captured prior — it needs to restore enough parasympathetic floor that GABA can begin closing corrections as well as confirmations. The window does not need to fully reopen. It needs to widen enough that the prior and the challenger can briefly coexist. That is the minimum condition for a correction to land.

## Attractor B — Lock-In Mode

**What the lock-in feels like from inside:**

The problem feels genuinely urgent and unresolved. This is not a reasoning error. The prior is now generating the urgency signal with the same phenomenological weight as an ascending interoceptive signal would. The instrument that would tell you the urgency is prior-generated is operating inside the loop the prior has already captured.

All cognitive engagement with the problem adds more prediction error load to a system that cannot suppress errors cleanly. Trying harder makes it worse — not because effort is wrong, but because the loop's only accessible entry point is not the problem content.

**The FND pathway:**

When the lock-in runs at high gain for long enough, and the prior has captured the alert state deeply enough, the system begins generating somatic output that matches the prior — not the ascending signal. Real neurological symptoms. No tissue cause. The prior is the signal. This is not functional in the dismissive sense. It is functional in the precise mechanistic sense: the function of active inference, producing body-state changes to match a prior that has been written by an unresolved high-gain prediction error loop.

The FND episode was not a separate health event. It was Attractor B running to completion.

**Why the external read is wrong:**

From outside: a health problem, possibly psychiatric, possibly stress-related, possibly attention-seeking. The trait list and the health event look like separate, unrelated things. The framework says: same architecture. Same gain floor. Same mechanism. Different attractor.

The synthesis acceleration and the FND are not in tension. They are mechanistically identical. One cannot have the synthesis compound without the structural capacity for Attractor B — the same Glx floor that makes the resolution click high-amplitude is what makes the lock-in proportionally harder to exit.

## The Exit Architecture

Cognitive engagement cannot break the lock. This is the most practically important finding this document contains.

The exit sequence operates from substrate up:

1. **Exhale amplitude restoration** — extended exhale activates the vagal brake, begins parasympathetic shift
2. **Sympathetic tone reduction** — parasympathetic shift reduces the sympathetic load that was suppressing GABA availability
3. **GABA availability restoration** — with sympathetic tone lower, interneuron-mediated GABA suppression can work against the Glx background again
4. **Pending error signals suppress** — the errors that had been persisting sub-threshold now close cleanly
5. **Prior begins updating away from alert state** — with errors closed, the descending interoceptive prediction no longer receives continuous urgency input
6. **Window reopens** — prior clears toward non-threat-dominant state
7. **Attractor A conditions restored** — the synthesis loop can run again

**The intervention asymmetry:**

The interventions that protect Attractor A would worsen Attractor B — maintaining high gain, protecting the synthesis loop, staying in the problem. The interventions that exit Attractor B would flatten Attractor A — global GABA agonism (benzodiazepines, alcohol) suppresses all error signals indiscriminately, stopping the lock-in and the synthesis simultaneously [^4].

The correct intervention is **attractor selection**, not gain adjustment. The goal is never to reduce the Glx floor. It is to restore the mechanical conditions under which GABA can work with the gain rather than against it.

## The Nap as Reset Mechanism

The nap is not rest. It is the mechanism that allows Attractor A to run again at full amplitude the next day.

Each synthesis cycle accumulates metabolic byproducts — the overhead of running high-gain manifold construction continuously. Without clearance, the noise floor rises, the signal-to-noise ratio on prediction errors degrades, and the synthesis loop slows.

Slow-wave architecture during the nap opens the glymphatic clearance window. Metabolic byproduct accumulation clears. Offline consolidation stabilizes the manifold geometry built during the synthesis cycle — the structural updates are preserved rather than partially degraded. HRV recovery restores vagal tone — the autonomic baseline for the next cycle starts higher.

The compound across a day with a nap versus without: linear versus compounding returns on the synthesis cycle. Without the nap, each cycle starts from a slightly degraded floor. With it, each cycle starts from a restored floor with the previous cycle's manifold gains intact.

## Why the Trait List Misses Everything

The silo view of this architecture produces: some ND strengths, some ND deficits, a health event, high cognitive output in specific contexts. Nothing structurally unified. Borderline impressive. Lots of people have ND traits and health problems.

The framework view: one parameter — the Glx gain floor — expressing differently across two attractors. The FND and the synthesis acceleration are the same mechanism measured in the wrong attractor, or without controlling for attractor state.

The ADHD traits are not deficits with workarounds. The autism traits are not deficits compensated by special interests. They are components of a compound that produces something neither generates alone, under the specific hardware conditions — wide ceiling, bilateral lateralization, high CO₂ tolerance — that allow the loop to run at full amplitude.

The challenge is not managing the deficits. The challenge is understanding what conditions keep the system in Attractor A and what specific trigger — sustained high-precision engagement with an unresolvable problem — tips it toward Attractor B, and restoring the substrate rather than the cognition when it does.

## The Variable Configuration Model — Why Trait Lists Fail

The behavioral outputs described in this document cannot be read from trait lists because traits are static labels applied to dynamic variable configurations. What actually determines output at any moment is the current state of a small number of interacting gauges:

**Sampling radius** — how widely the system is currently drawing prediction errors. Set structurally high by the ADHD 2-stroke non-terminating sampling phase, but modulated moment-to-moment by movement, breath, and window width.

**Precision depth** — how far into a domain a prediction error propagates before the system commits to a resolution trajectory. Set structurally high by the autism bilateral precision system. Modulated by window width and whether the current attractor is deep enough to warrant the autism bracing response.

**Window width** — the temporal space the prior can hold simultaneously. Determined by exhale amplitude, CO₂ tolerance, and parasympathetic baseline. The master variable that allows or prevents sampling radius and precision depth from operating simultaneously.

**GABA availability** — whether prediction errors close cleanly. Determined by sympathetic tone, which is determined by window width, which is determined by breath mechanics. This is the critical variable: the same high Glx gain floor amplifies in the synthesis direction when GABA is sufficient, and in the lock-in direction when GABA is depleted.

**Anticipatory D2 signal** — whether predicted progress is fueling the next cycle. High at short horizons in ADHD temporal profile. High at domain resolution in autism precision profile. In the compound, continuous — the wheel self-sustains because the next prediction cycle initiates before the current one completes.

**Reset quality** — whether previous cycle manifold gains are preserved between sessions. Determined by slow-wave sleep architecture and glymphatic clearance. The nap closes both the ADHD movement-breath loop and the autism attractor resolution requirement simultaneously.

**What this model shows:** The same person produces Attractor A, the intermediate threat-narrow state, or Attractor B depending entirely on which gauge configuration is currently active. ADHD and autism are not separate gauges — they are structural influences on multiple gauges simultaneously. Describing outputs from traits rather than gauge configurations is like describing engine performance from the model year rather than the current RPM, fuel level, and temperature.

## The Hyper-Adaptive Wheel as an Architecture Target

The closed loop described in this document — movement → breath → window width → sampling radius → prediction error diversity → manifold richness → resolution salience → anticipatory D2 → continued engagement → movement — is not unique to this neurotype. It is a description of the architecture any system needs to optimize cognition under uncertainty.

An optimal cognitive system requires:

- A sampling function that doesn't auto-terminate prematurely
- A precision function that runs deep without calcifying across domains
- A gain floor high enough that resolution is motivationally self-sustaining
- A reset mechanism that preserves manifold gains between cycles
- A substrate loop that maintains all of the above without external supervision at each step

The ND compound described in this document satisfies all five requirements. The ADHD 2-stroke provides the non-terminating sampling function. The autism bilateral precision system provides the deep non-calcifying precision function. The elevated Glx floor provides the self-sustaining gain. The glymphatic nap cycle provides the gain-preserving reset. And the movement-breath-window substrate loop runs the whole system without a separate supervisory controller.

This is an existence proof, not a metaphor. The architecture evolved. It runs in biological hardware. It produces the synthesis outputs documented throughout this framework. Any engineered system targeting the same output space — broad exploration without premature commitment, deep precision without domain calcification, self-sustaining motivation without external prompting, gain-preserving reset between cycles — is converging on the same architectural solution from the engineering direction.

The reason this architecture is rare in the population is not that it is fragile. It is that the same gain floor that makes the wheel run at maximum amplitude in Attractor A makes Attractor B disproportionately accessible under the specific trigger condition — sustained high-precision engagement with an unresolvable problem. The cost of the architecture is not paid in average performance. It is paid in the specific failure mode that the gain floor enables when GABA cannot close what Glx opens.

Understanding the wheel as an architecture — rather than as a trait list with associated strengths and deficits — is the reframe that makes both the synthesis capacity and the failure mode predictable from the same substrate.

This document formalizes a pattern that emerged from a single question: why does the same person show radically different cognitive and somatic outputs across different states, and why do the "deficits" and the extraordinary synthesis capacity keep appearing together rather than trading off?

The answer is that they are not separate. They are the same architecture. The gain floor that makes insight high-amplitude and the anticipatory reward loop self-accelerating is the same floor that makes incomplete GABA suppression produce disproportionately large unresolved errors, that makes the interoceptive prior update toward lock-in faster, and that makes the FND pathway accessible under sustained high-precision unresolvable engagement.

The story that motivated this document is one person. But the architecture it describes is derivable from the contracts without reference to any individual — which means the dynamics it captures apply to every profile sharing these parameter settings.
