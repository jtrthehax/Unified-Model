## Session 3 Results

### Gap 4: Vestibular stimulation and autonomic function
**Status: PARTIAL CONFIRMED — rhythmic specificity is the key finding**

**Literature found:**
- PubMed: Transient HR decrease with GVS; "average intervals between normal heartbeats recorded with stimulation resemble parasympathetic cardiac function induced by auricular vagal nerve stimulation." Direct comparison to vagal stimulation confirmed.
- ScienceDirect: HF component of HRV increased during sinusoidal GVS. Sinusoidal GVS modifies muscle sympathetic nerve activity. Noisy random GVS in elderly showed no HRV effect.
- 2025 review: GVS employed to alleviate autonomic dysfunction in Parkinson's disease. GVS described as "complementary neuromodulation strategy to treat autonomic dysregulation" — framework framing confirmed in clinical literature.

**Critical specificity finding:**
Rhythmic sinusoidal vestibular input produces autonomic effects resembling vagal stimulation. Random noisy GVS does not. Frequency and rhythm matter — not vestibular stimulation in general but patterned rhythmic vestibular input specifically. This is exactly what the framework predicts: rhythmic movement is the regulatory input, not movement per se.

**Extension to ADHD movement-seeking:**
ADHD hyperactivity isn't random movement — it has rhythm and pattern. The system is seeking the specific vestibular input frequency that produces autonomic regulation, not stimulation generally. This reframes hyperactivity as frequency-specific regulatory seeking. Removing rhythmic movement in classroom settings removes the frequency-specific input the regulatory system requires — not stimulation management, input deprivation.

**Fixed chair mechanism clarified:**
Fixed chair prevents vestibular escape but also forces the system to find regulation through other channels — breath, posture, interoception. This is the training effect. The system learns to regulate without the vestibular crutch because avoidance is prevented. Swivel chair permits continuous rhythmic self-correction which masks the regulatory deficit rather than building capacity through it.

**What still needs testing:**
The specific frequency bands of rhythmic movement that produce optimal autonomic regulatory effects — and whether they match the natural ADHD movement patterns. If ADHD hyperactivity frequency-matches the optimal vestibular-autonomic input band, the 2-stroke engine mechanism and the vestibular regulatory seeking are the same phenomenon described at different levels.

**Chain completeness:** Vestibular-NTS-autonomic pathway: load-bearing. Rhythmic specificity requirement: scaffolding. ADHD frequency-specific seeking: predictive.

### Gap 11: Interoceptive accuracy as transdiagnostic treatment predictor
**Status: ✓ CONFIRMED — mechanism load-bearing; treatment prediction test explicitly called for**

**Literature found:**
- PLOS Computational Biology (2020) + Replication (2023): Bayesian computational model reveals transdiagnostic failure to adapt interoceptive precision estimates across depression, anxiety, eating disorders, and substance use disorders. Replicated in independent sample.
- Key finding: During inspiratory breath-holding (interoceptive perturbation amplifying afferent signal), healthy controls increased precision-weighting of ascending cardiac signals. All psychiatric patient groups did not — they maintained flat precision estimates regardless of afferent signal amplitude.
- The failure is specifically precision weighting adaptation, not interoceptive accuracy per se — the mechanism matches the COG↔INTERO contract exactly.
- Replication study conclusion: "Future studies should examine the utility of interoceptive precision measures in predicting treatment outcomes."
- Nature Scientific Reports: Healthy participants showed performance improvements across interoceptive perturbation conditions; those with depression/anxiety and substance use disorder showed minimal improvement — transdiagnostic pattern replicated.
- Transdiagnostic clinical sample (N=258 patients + 67 controls): Patients differed from controls in HRV, cardiac processing, and interoceptive confidence — confirming the physiological substrate.

**Confirmed chain:**
Failure to update interoceptive precision estimates in response to amplified afferent signals → transdiagnostic marker of COG↔INTERO failure → present across depression, anxiety, eating disorders, substance use disorders simultaneously → replicated in independent samples

**Framework prediction status:**
The Bayesian framing in the literature matches COG↔INTERO contract language precisely. The mechanism is confirmed. The treatment prediction test — that interoceptive precision updating capacity predicts treatment response better than diagnosis — is explicitly called for in the literature and has not yet been run. The framework identifies the next study.

**Clinical implication:**
The breath-hold interoceptive perturbation task is a candidate for pre-treatment stratification. Patients who fail to increase precision weighting during breath-hold have the precision weighting apparatus broken — they need upstream contract intervention (AUTO→MOD→COG↔INTERO) before cognitive-level treatment can work. This is the mechanism behind treatment resistance the framework predicts.

**Chain completeness:** Transdiagnostic precision weighting failure: load-bearing, replicated. Treatment prediction test: predictive — mechanism confirmed, test called for in literature, not yet run.

### Gap 14: Middle ear muscle regulation in autism / SSP as predictive marker
**Status: PARTIAL CONFIRMED — mechanism confirmed as SSP foundation; predictive marker not yet tested**

**Literature found:**
- SSP neurophysiological background document: SSP was developed to rehabilitate middle ear muscle function specifically. Mechanism is acoustic stimulation training middle ear muscles to process human speech frequencies — exactly the framework mechanism.
- SSP patent (US): Middle-ear transfer function is explicitly listed alongside HRV and RSA as measurable pre/post treatment characteristics. The measurement tool already exists in the patent.
- PMC review 2025: SSP targets VVC through filtered music, producing parasympathetic activation and HRV improvement. Compared directly with HRV biofeedback as non-invasive vagal interventions.
- Clinical trials: SSP used across autism, sensory processing disorder, trauma, and dysautonomia — the diagnostic breadth confirms it's acting on a transdiagnostic mechanism (VVC/middle ear) not a diagnosis-specific one.

**What's confirmed:**
- SSP mechanism is exactly the middle ear muscle → VVC → social engagement chain the framework describes
- Middle ear transfer function is already in the protocol as a measurable variable
- HRV improvement from SSP is confirmed
- SSP works transdiagnostically because it targets the VVC hardware not a specific diagnosis

**What still needs testing:**
Pre-treatment acoustic reflex testing as a predictor of SSP response. The measurement tool exists. The study hasn't been run. The framework predicts: acoustic reflex threshold before treatment should predict SSP response magnitude — patients with more impaired middle ear muscle regulation should show larger response to SSP if the mechanism is correct.

**Chain completeness:** Middle ear muscle → VVC → social engagement: load-bearing (SSP is built on it). Acoustic reflex as predictive pre-treatment marker: predictive — mechanism confirmed, test not yet run.

### Gap 15: Digital communication and HRV deficit
**Status: PARTIAL CONFIRMED — direction confirmed; mechanism attribution confounded; minority invariant identified**

**Literature found:**
- PLOS ONE (2020): COVID lockdown HRV study. 80% of participants showed increased HR and decreased RMSSD during lockdown; 20% showed opposite (decreased HR, increased RMSSD) and rated lockdown positively. HRV correlated significantly with subjective wellbeing across all phases.
- Longitudinal data comparing pre-lockdown baseline, during lockdown, and recovery period.
- Confounds: Lockdown simultaneously changed exercise patterns, work stress, sleep, and social interaction — cannot cleanly isolate digital-primary social communication as the causal variable.

**The minority invariant:**
20% showed HRV improvement during lockdown. The framework predicts exactly who they are: people for whom in-person social demands impose more regulatory cost than co-regulatory benefit — atypical VVC calibration, ND profiles, those for whom digital stripping of the social channel reduces processing load rather than depleting co-regulatory input. That specific sub-group analysis can be run against the existing lockdown HRV data.

**What's confirmed:**
Direction: forced digital-primary social period correlates with HRV decline in majority of population.

**What's confounded:**
Mechanism attribution — lockdown changed too many variables simultaneously to isolate the SOC↔AUTO channel specifically.

**What the minority invariant suggests:**
The 20% who improved during lockdown are the test of the SOC↔AUTO digital stripping hypothesis from the other direction. If they show the regulatory profile the framework predicts (atypical VVC, ND traits, high in-person social processing demand), the digital stripping mechanism is confirmed from both sides simultaneously.

**Chain completeness:** SOC↔AUTO digital stripping → HRV decline direction: scaffolding. Mechanism isolation: Type C for clean study. Minority invariant identification: predictive test available in existing dataset.

## Meta-observation (Session 3)

The interoceptive precision finding is the most important for clinical translation. The Bayesian framing in the computational psychiatry literature matches the COG↔INTERO contract so precisely that the framework and the research are describing the same mechanism in different language. The breath-hold perturbation task is a candidate for immediate clinical use — it requires no technology, measures the exact mechanism the framework identifies as the source of treatment resistance, and is transdiagnostic.

The vestibular rhythmic specificity finding refines the ADHD mechanism. It's not that the 2-stroke engine needs movement — it needs rhythmic movement at specific frequencies. This makes the intervention more precise: the therapeutic target is specific vestibular input frequency, not movement in general.
