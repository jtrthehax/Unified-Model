# Framework Gap Analysis
## Regulatory Architecture Framework — Systematic Gap Review
*Generated after addition of NOCI↔COG, GLYPH↔AUTO, and the Temporal Layer to minimal_core.md*

## Overview

This document maps all identified gaps across the framework: unresolved mechanistic links within existing contracts, cross-contract interactions named but not assembled, population-specific mechanisms incomplete, structural absences, and predictions ready for empirical assembly from existing literature.

Organized for prioritization. Chain completeness ratings used throughout: Load-bearing / Scaffolding / Predictive / Adjacent.

## Category 1 — Unresolved Links Within Existing Contracts

### AUTO↔MOD
- **Kumbhaka suspended carrier state:** What NTS and ECS do when RSA suspends rather than oscillates. Explicitly marked highest-priority unresolved link in the contract. No research has studied ECS tone or precision gain during maintained breath retention in trained practitioners. Freediving population is the closest proxy. CB2 receptor trial in freedivers was recruiting — status unknown.
- **RSA phase specificity for ECS modulation:** Whether inhale-phase vs exhale-phase RSA dynamics produce different ECS signals at NTS level. The mechanism by which breathing ratio specifically — not just total HRV — modulates the contract. High priority, not yet assembled.
- **Hemidiaphragm asymmetry as deliberate lateralization control:** Anatomy established, deliberate asymmetric application unstudied. Requires simultaneous spirometry, EEG frontal asymmetry, and postural angle measurement in practitioners with demonstrated skill.
- **Oculomotor-vagal pathway specificity:** Brainstem proximity of oculomotor and vagal nuclei documented, specific pathway by which gaze stabilization modulates vagal tone needs direct investigation.
- **CO2 tolerance training as peripheral vs central recalibration:** Whether threshold shift is at carotid body or NTS. Phenomenologically established, mechanistically unresolved.

### MICRO↔AUTO
- **Individual SCFA receptor density variation:** FFAR2/FFAR3 expression on EECs varies between individuals, likely determining the gain of the MICRO→AUTO signal. The individual variation parameter for this contract — not characterized.
- **Neuropod SCFA signaling specificity:** Fast path sugar sensing confirmed via neuropod cells, whether SCFAs specifically use the fast neuropod path remains inferential.
- **AUTO→MICRO timescale in humans:** How quickly vagal tone change produces measurable microbiome composition shift. Direction established, human timescales uncharacterized.
- **Optimal microbiome diversity threshold:** At what point does diversity degradation produce measurable HRV impairment. Clinical threshold undefined.

### MOD↔COG
- **ADHD temporal horizon specificity:** Normal precision weighting at short temporal horizons, impaired at long horizons. Marked predictive. Delay discounting literature probably contains relevant data — not yet assembled into this framing.
- **Individual CB1 density as master individual difference variable:** If ECS is gain controller for all three monoamine systems, CB1 receptor density variation should predict psychiatric vulnerability across multiple domains simultaneously. Not assembled as cross-domain predictor.
- **Psychedelic therapeutic window mechanism:** What determines whether post-psychedelic reorganization produces stable benefit vs relapse. Model predicts it depends on regulatory environment during the window — not yet formally tested.
- **ECS-monoamine cross-domain correlation:** Prediction that ECS tone markers should correlate simultaneously across all three monoamine domains. Studies look at ECS and one monoamine at a time. Cross-domain measurement not done.

### COG↔INTERO
- **Pre-operative interoceptive screening for FND risk:** Vulnerability profile confirmed in FND literature, surgical onset mechanism confirmed, pre-operative screening protocol not yet tested. Prediction 6 is specific and actionable — interoceptive accuracy plus stress reactivity profiling before elective surgery, followed by graduated body-mapping preparation for high-risk patients.
- **Post-surgical FND as mechanistically distinct from chronic FND:** Acute discontinuity mechanism vs chronic drift mechanism. Framework derivation confirmed by clinical pattern but the two mechanisms haven't been formally distinguished in the literature or in clinical protocol.

### INTERO↔DEV
- **Window-specific adversity producing window-specific interoceptive profiles:** Direction established but specific precision weighting patterns from specific developmental windows not assembled.
- **Transgenerational epigenetic load predicting regulatory baseline independently of individual experience:** Testable with multigenerational methylation profiling. Not yet done.
- **Palatal architecture → nasal cycle → regulatory capacity full chain:** Component links load-bearing, assembled chain needs explicit study with simultaneous rhinomanometry and HRV monitoring.

### NOCI↔COG
- **Pre-injury HRV predicting chronification risk:** Highest-priority prediction from this contract. Not yet tested in prospective surgical cohorts. Pre-injury HRV should predict chronic pain development better than injury severity, age, or psychological history.
- **TRPV1 dual-role interference:** Whether peripheral inflammatory sensitization of TRPV1 affects vagal afferent TRPV1 regulatory function. Genuinely unknown — gap identification rather than assembled chain.
- **ND mast cell threshold → peripheral nociceptor sensitization → ND pain sensitivity:** Component links load-bearing, assembly novel and untested. Predicts lower mechanical pain thresholds at non-sensitized locations in ND-MCAS populations vs neurotypical controls matched for central sensitization markers.
- **Interoceptive attention as volume knob maintenance mechanism:** Identified in clinical observation but not yet formally described in the contract. Directing focal interoceptive attention toward a body region amplifies signal from that region through precision weighting, which updates the threat prior, which generates active inference tension, which produces more signal, which maintains attention. This is a third chronic pain maintenance mechanism distinct from peripheral sensitization and behavioral avoidance — purely attentional, requiring no tissue input. ND profiles with higher baseline interoceptive precision weighting may be specifically vulnerable because directed interoceptive attention produces stronger signal amplification per unit of attention. The FND jaw case is the clean demonstration: attention → amplified signal → tension generated through active inference → proprioceptive confirmation of prior → attention maintained. No injury, no inflammation, no avoidance required. Therapeutic implication: interoceptive defusion rather than interoceptive attention — peripheral rather than focal interoceptive awareness to maintain the anchor function without feeding the amplification loop. Standard mindfulness instruction to bring awareness to body sensations may be contraindicated for this specific mechanism in ND chronic pain profiles.

### GLYPH↔AUTO
- **Respiratory muscle strength predicting CSF flow velocity:** Scaffolding, not yet directly measured with phase-contrast MRI across respiratory fitness levels.
- **Yoga practitioners showing lower amyloid accumulation:** Predictive, prospective longitudinal confirmation required.
- **Pre-sleep HRV predicting slow wave proportion and clearance outcome:** Predictive, not yet measured as deliberate intervention target.
- **Kumbhaka → CSF pulsatility dynamics:** What breath retention does to the respiratory component of CSF drive. Not addressed in either AUTO↔MOD or GLYPH↔AUTO. During retention the respiratory CSF pulsatility component suspends while arterial pulsatility continues. Net effect on clearance in trained practitioners genuinely unknown. Sitting at the intersection of AUTO↔MOD, GLYPH↔AUTO, and the physics layer simultaneously.

## Category 2 — Cross-Contract Interactions Named But Not Assembled

### Estrogen-Histamine Interaction (MET↔IMMUNE × INTERO↔DEV)
Named explicitly in MET↔IMMUNE as a clinical complication for ND women. The plasticity window opens during high-estrogen phase through perineuronal net dissolution per INTERO↔DEV. Simultaneously estrogen elevates mast cell reactivity and impairs histamine degradation via reduced DAO enzyme activity. For ND women with MCAS these two effects arrive in the same menstrual phase — the therapeutic access window and the highest immune reactivity window coincide.

Neither contract accounts for which effect dominates in clinical practice. No protocol exists for navigating this interaction. The framework has all component pieces but no integrated account. Probably the most clinically significant unaddressed gap for a specific population in the entire framework.

### SOC↔AUTO × NOCI↔COG — Therapeutic Presence as Analgesia
Named in NOCI↔COG operating states as a one-liner. Regulated social presence → vagal tone → ECS → nociceptive gain reduction. The mechanism is complete. The clinical implications — therapeutic touch, therapeutic relationship, co-regulation as a pain intervention — are not developed. This is mechanistically distinct from placebo and from distraction. Currently has no dedicated home in either contract.

### MICRO↔AUTO × NOCI↔COG — TRPV1 Dual Role
Named in NOCI↔COG Link 7 as adjacent. Peripheral inflammatory sensitization potentially affecting vagal TRPV1 regulatory function. If chronic pain states sensitize TRPV1 broadly, the gut-vagal regulatory signal may be distorted simultaneously. The pain state is potentially degrading the MICRO↔AUTO regulatory channel through the same molecular mechanism it uses to generate peripheral sensitization. No contract assembles this.

### AUTO↔MOD × GLYPH↔AUTO — Kumbhaka CSF Dynamics
Not addressed in either contract. During breath retention the respiratory component of CSF pulsatility suspends while intrathoracic pressure holds at a fixed set point. Arterial pulsatility continues. The net effect on CSF flow during extended retention in trained practitioners is genuinely unknown. May produce a qualitatively different clearance state than either normal breathing or slow wave sleep. Novel prediction sitting at intersection of two contracts and the physics layer. Testable with phase-contrast MRI in trained practitioners during extended retention.

### MET↔IMMUNE × NOCI↔COG — Dietary Glucose → Pain Intervention Sequence
Assembled mechanistically in NOCI↔COG Link 4. The dietary management implication for ND pain presentations is stated as a failure mode. But the full intervention sequence — dietary glucose reduction first to reduce mast cell activation, mast cell stabilization second to reduce peripheral sensitization, vagal tone restoration third to reduce cognitive gain, graded exposure fourth in reduced-gain state — is not assembled as a clinical protocol anywhere. The sequence matters and is not explicit.

### GLYPH↔AUTO × INTERO↔DEV — Early Life Clearance Deficit Writing Developmental Baseline
Named in GLYPH↔AUTO adjacent contracts. Chronic clearance insufficiency during developmental windows may alter the neuroinflammatory baseline written into the DEV contract. Early childhood sleep architecture quality — and its determinants including breathing mechanics, adenotonsil hypertrophy, and palatal architecture — affecting lifelong regulatory setpoints through accumulated clearance deficit. INTERO↔DEV describes developmental window setpoint establishment but doesn't account for the neuroinflammatory substrate from clearance failure that may be part of what's being written.

### Circadian × IMMUNE↔AUTO — Phase-Dependent CAP Responsiveness
The 4-week HRV warning window in RA is phase-dependent. Nocturnal inflammatory peaks when vagal tone is lowest. CAP responsiveness varies across the 24-hour cycle. The temporal layer names this as a lateral note in IMMUNE↔AUTO but the specific mechanism — why nocturnal inflammation peaks, what the circadian amplitude of CAP function looks like, and how this interacts with the ND delayed phase profile — is not assembled. ND individuals with delayed phase may have their lowest CAP responsiveness arriving at a different clock time than neurotypical individuals, meaning standard treatment timing recommendations may be systematically mistimed for this population.

### Circadian × MET↔AUTO — Insulin Sensitivity Windows and Delayed Phase
Insulin sensitivity has a strong circadian profile. MET↔AUTO describes the carotid body glucose-insulin-autonomic chain but doesn't integrate the circadian timing of that chain's amplitude. For delayed phase profiles the metabolic window arrives later. Eating at clock times recommended by standard nutritional advice may be in the equivalent of the biological middle of the night in terms of insulin sensitivity for delayed phase profiles. Standard eat-breakfast-early recommendations may be actively counterproductive. Not assembled.

### Circadian × MOD↔COG — Dopamine and ECS Circadian Peaks and Delayed Phase
Dopamine and ECS availability have circadian release patterns. MOD↔COG describes the precision gain mechanism but not when that gain is maximally available. For delayed phase profiles the dopamine and ECS peaks arrive at clock times that standard scheduling treats as wind-down time. The mismatch between when cognitive performance is demanded and when the neurochemical substrate for it peaks is probably a significant but unmeasured contributor to ND performance variability. Not assembled.

### AUTO↔MOD × COG↔INTERO — Interoceptive Attention as Pain Volume Knob
The interoceptive attention switching mechanism described in AUTO↔MOD Link 8 interacts with the pain prior maintenance mechanism in NOCI↔COG in a way neither contract fully describes. Focal interoceptive attention directed at a body region increases precision weighting on signals from that region — this is the mechanism by which interoceptive attention amplifies rather than merely observes. In chronic pain or FND, directed interoceptive attention toward the affected region feeds the prior through the same precision weighting apparatus that therapeutic interoceptive awareness is supposed to correct. The intervention implication — peripheral rather than focal interoceptive awareness, defusion rather than attention — sits at the intersection of both contracts and is described in neither.

## Category 3 — Population-Specific Mechanisms Incomplete

### ND Women with MCAS Across the Menstrual Cycle
The most incomplete population-specific treatment in the framework. Cyclically varying therapeutic access profile — plasticity window and immune reactivity window coinciding in high-estrogen phase — that no contract currently accounts for. Standard approach of using high-estrogen phase for therapeutic work may be wrong for this population specifically. All component pieces exist across MET↔IMMUNE, INTERO↔DEV, and NOCI↔COG. No integrated account.

### ND Individuals with Chronic Pain
NOCI↔COG derives the peripheral sensitization mechanism from MCAS. The clinical implication — address peripheral driver before central intervention — is stated as a failure mode. The full intervention sequence isn't assembled: dietary glucose management first, mast cell stabilization second, vagal tone restoration third, graded exposure in reduced-gain state fourth. Additionally the interoceptive attention maintenance loop — ND high baseline interoceptive precision making focal attention a stronger volume knob amplifier — is identified but not integrated into the protocol.

### ND Individuals with Delayed Phase — Daily Protocol
The temporal layer describes the bootstrapping stack. The full daily protocol for delayed phase ND profile isn't assembled: sequencing strong zeitgebers, protecting against evening entrainment threshold crossings, phase-relative intervention timing, managing social jet lag without forcing the phase. The pieces exist in the temporal layer section but not as a coherent actionable protocol.

### Post-Surgical FND Risk Population
COG↔INTERO Prediction 6 describes the screening protocol. The preparation intervention for identified high-risk patients — graduated interoceptive body-mapping before surgery — is named but not developed mechanistically. What this preparation involves, at what timescale, and what outcome measures would confirm it worked is not assembled.

### Aging Population with Declining Respiratory Mechanics
GLYPH↔AUTO describes the pump failure trajectory. The intervention — respiratory maintenance as primary neurological aging prevention — isn't developed as a protocol. Minimum effective dose of breathing practice for clearance preservation across aging, what specifically needs to be maintained, and how to distinguish pump maintenance from clearance window maintenance as separate intervention targets is not assembled.

## Category 4 — Structural Gaps

### No Contract for Acute Stress Response Resolution
The framework describes chronic sympathetic dominance extensively. The acute stress response — fight/flight activation, its natural resolution through physical discharge, and what happens when the resolution is incomplete — has no dedicated treatment. The polyvagal three-tier hierarchy in SOC↔AUTO covers activation states but resolution mechanism and consequences of incomplete resolution aren't assembled. This is probably where trauma onset mechanism lives more completely than in any existing contract. The difference between acute stress that resolves and acute stress that initiates a chronic attractor state is the most clinically important transition in the framework and it's not formally described.

### No Contract for Sleep Architecture as a Regulatory Layer
GLYPH↔AUTO addresses sleep as the clearance window. The temporal layer addresses circadian phase. Sleep architecture itself — stage sequence, what determines stage transitions, what disrupts them, regulatory consequences of architectural disruption beyond clearance — has no dedicated home. REM sleep and memory consolidation, slow wave and REM proportion relationships, architectural disruption consequences for contracts above — named in fragments, not assembled.

### No Contract for Proprioception and Movement
The framework covers interoception through COG↔INTERO and vestibular input through AUTO↔MOD. Proprioception — continuous body position and movement signal running in parallel with interoception — has no dedicated treatment. Movement as regulatory input beyond its metabolic and ECS effects is absent. Explains why specific movement practices produce regulatory effects not fully explained by metabolic or breathing components alone. The graded exposure mechanism in NOCI↔COG depends on proprioceptive corrective signal but the proprioceptive channel isn't formally described anywhere.

### NOCI↔COG Not Represented as Convergence Contract in Table
The contracts table in minimal_core.md shows bilateral crossovers. NOCI↔COG is structurally a convergence contract drawing from four upstream contracts simultaneously. The table entry doesn't capture this structural difference. A convergence contracts category or notation system would make the architectural distinction visible.

## Category 6 — Empirical Assembly Classification

Every gap has been classified into three buckets based on what is required to address it:

**Bucket A — Assemblable from existing literature:** Data exists in published studies or accessible datasets. No new data collection required. The contribution is reframing, reanalysis, or synthesis through the framework's lens.

**Bucket B — Needs targeted study, existing methods:** Measurement tools exist, populations are accessible, study design is clear. New data required but no new technology or infrastructure. Someone with the right equipment and access could run these.

**Bucket C — Genuine future studies:** Requires new research infrastructure, novel measurement approaches, invasive methods not currently applied to these populations, or longitudinal designs that don't yet exist.

The primary value of this classification: Bucket A gaps can be addressed by anyone with literature access and analytical skill. Bucket B gaps identify specific studies the framework calls for — these are the predictions most useful to share with researchers who have relevant infrastructure. Bucket C gaps are explicitly acknowledged as future work requiring resources not currently available.

## Notes on Framework Architecture

**Convergence contracts as a structural category:** NOCI↔COG and GLYPH↔AUTO are both convergence contracts — multiple upstream contracts feeding a single output simultaneously. This architecture is probably more common than the current bilateral crossover model assumes. When identifying new gaps, ask first whether the phenomenon is a bilateral crossover or a convergence point — the structural difference determines whether a new contract or an extension of existing contracts is the right treatment.

**The interoceptive attention amplification mechanism** identified in the ND chronic pain clinical observation extends beyond pain. Any interoceptive channel can be amplified through focal attention in ND profiles with higher baseline interoceptive precision weighting. The same mechanism that maintains the pain prior through attention may be operating in anxiety (focal interoceptive attention on cardiac signals), panic disorder (attention amplifying respiratory prediction error), and health anxiety (attention maintaining threat priors about multiple body regions simultaneously). The defusion intervention — peripheral rather than focal interoceptive awareness — may be transdiagnostically relevant across all precision-weighting-dependent conditions.

**The empirical pass** should prioritize predictions where the data exists and the reframing is the contribution — these produce framework validation at low cost. Novel predictions requiring new research are higher value but longer timescale. The framework's most immediate contribution to existing literature is the reframing of known findings through the precision weighting and pressure mechanics lens rather than new empirical claims.
