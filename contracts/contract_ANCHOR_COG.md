## Mechanistic Chain

### Link 1 — Mechanical anchor → RSA amplitude

Diaphragm excursion depth, exhale completeness, and thoracic symmetry jointly determine RSA amplitude, HRV bandwidth, and vagal afferent signal quality. Breathing is HRV at the mechanical level — the pressure differential generated per breath cycle is the direct physical driver of the oscillation that RSA measures. A strong anchor produces a strong oscillation. A weak anchor compresses the oscillation amplitude before any downstream cognitive effect is possible. This is the entry point of the chain.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing. Established across respiratory physiology and HRV literature.

---

### Link 2 — RSA amplitude → prediction window width

The oscillation range determines how far the prediction window can open on inhale. Inhale opens the prediction window; exhale narrows it. This is the breath-gated cognitive cycle described in COG↔REASON. The critical addition this contract makes is that the *range* of the oscillation is mechanically constrained upstream. If the oscillation amplitude is compressed — through incomplete exhale, shallow breathing, or asymmetric diaphragm loading — the window cannot widen fully on inhale regardless of cognitive intent. The mechanical ceiling is set before the cognitive layer acts.

This is the mechanical origin of tunnel vision, recursive thinking, rigid priors, loss of curiosity, and binary reasoning under load. These are not primarily cognitive failures. They are outputs of a mechanically compressed oscillation.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing via RSA-cognition literature; the specific amplitude-ceiling mechanism is scaffolding — mechanistically coherent, direct measurement pending.

---

### Link 3 — Proprioceptive confirmation → precision gain stability

Bilateral rhythmic proprioceptive input provides a second anchoring signal operating in parallel with respiratory mechanics. Clean proprioceptive error signals entrain the prediction cycle, generating rhythmic cerebellar prediction-confirmation streams that stabilize precision gain. When proprioception is degraded — through mechanoreceptor signal noise, connective tissue laxity, or motor forward model uncertainty — prediction error becomes noisy, sympathetic load rises, precision gain spikes, and the window narrows independently of respiratory mechanics. This is the mechanism behind ND motor-planning patterns and load-dependent cognitive switching in populations with proprioceptive signal quality deficits.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Mechanistically complete via PROPRIO↔AUTO/COG. Requires direct measurement of precision gain modulation by proprioceptive signal quality.

---

### Link 4 — Interoceptive clarity → prior correctability

Interoceptive signal clarity is the third anchoring input. Prediction error from ascending interoceptive signals is what keeps priors correctable — the body-state signal that allows the generative model to update against incoming evidence rather than run forward on prior momentum. When interoception is degraded, priors become increasingly uncorrectable. The system cannot distinguish a prior-generated signal from a real ascending signal. Runaway narrative chains emerge. Fallacy groups 5 and 6 from COG↔REASON activate — interlocutor models simplify, confabulation increases, and the prior drives behavior rather than evidence.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing via COG↔INTERO. The specific interoceptive-clarity-to-prior-correctability link is confirmed in chronic pain and FND literature.

---

### Link 5 — Anchor failure → exhale-gate dominance

When all three anchoring inputs degrade simultaneously — incomplete exhale, proprioceptive noise, interoceptive degradation — the system enters exhale-gate dominance. The prediction window is frozen at its narrowest point. The oscillation that would normally allow sampling-mode reopening on inhale is absent because the mechanical substrate for it has collapsed. The cognitive layer experiences this as the inability to think clearly, change perspective, or update beliefs — but the cause is upstream of cognition entirely. Attributing this state to stress, emotion, or cognitive failure misidentifies the mechanism and directs intervention to the wrong layer.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Mechanistically complete. Prospective measurement of oscillation amplitude against reasoning flexibility required for direct confirmation.

## Failure Modes

**Misattribution of mechanical collapse to cognitive cause:**
Anchor failure produces a cognitive output — narrowed window, rigid priors, binary reasoning — that is phenomenologically indistinguishable from a cognitively-generated state. The person experiencing it and the clinician observing it both reach for cognitive explanations. The intervention that follows — cognitive restructuring, emotional processing, top-down reappraisal — targets the wrong layer. The mechanical substrate remains uncorrected and the cognitive intervention produces limited results, which then gets attributed to treatment resistance or insufficient motivation.

**External anchor dependency:**
Social co-regulation, therapist presence, and secure attachment all function as external anchors — they borrow prediction window width from another regulated nervous system and temporarily widen the local window. This is the ESLPM mechanism. The failure mode is when external anchoring substitutes for internal anchor development rather than scaffolding it. The window widens during the co-regulated interaction and collapses when the external anchor is removed. Dependency emerges not from attachment pathology but from an unrepaired internal anchor deficit that external regulation cannot permanently correct.

**Asymmetric diaphragm loading producing lateralized bias:**
Postural asymmetry that loads one hemidiaphragm more than the other produces asymmetric RSA and asymmetric hemispheric activation. The prediction window opens unevenly across hemispheres. Reasoning becomes laterally biased in ways that track the postural asymmetry rather than the cognitive content of the task. This is not a reasoning error at the cognitive level — it is a physical asymmetry in the pressure mechanics producing a systematic bias in which hemisphere drives the generative model at any given moment.

**Mindfulness without anchor repair:**
Mindfulness practice reduces prior precision to allow updating from incoming signals. When the anchor is degraded — respiratory mechanics compressed, proprioception noisy, interoception unclear — the signals available to update the prior are low quality. Opening precision weighting onto degraded signals does not produce accurate prior recalibration. It produces either amplified noise or continued prior dominance. Mindfulness without upstream anchor repair produces limited results for the same mechanistic reason as in COG↔INTERO: the signal being attended to is not clean enough to update the prior accurately.

**External architecture dependency without internal anchor development:**
The chair protocol is highly effective as a session architecture for high-demand cognitive work. The failure mode is identical to the external anchor dependency described for social co-regulation: if the passive load architecture substitutes for internal CO₂ tolerance development rather than scaffolding it, the window collapse returns completely when the architecture is absent — in standing conversations, meetings, phone calls, unexpected demand. The protocol should be used to access the wide-window state and accumulate experience from it, while CO₂ tolerance training proceeds in parallel to internalize the ceiling it provides.

Exhale completeness should correlate with prediction window width independent of breathing rate — because the oscillation amplitude, not the oscillation frequency, sets the cognitive ceiling. A slow shallow breath and a fast shallow breath should produce equivalent window narrowing; a slow complete breath should produce the widest window regardless of rate.

**Prediction 2 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
A single deliberate full exhale should produce measurable widening of prediction window performance on cognitive flexibility tasks more rapidly than any equivalent-duration cognitive intervention. The effect should be observable within one breath cycle. This is the most testable prediction the contract generates and the most diagnostic for the mechanical-vs-cognitive account.

**Prediction 3 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Asymmetric diaphragm loading — induced experimentally through sustained lateral posture — should produce measurable lateralized reasoning bias that correlates with the degree of postural asymmetry and resolves on symmetry restoration. This prediction separates the mechanical account from any cognitive or emotional account of reasoning asymmetry.

**Prediction 4 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Bilateral rhythmic movement should restore precision gain stability and widen prediction windows even in the complete absence of emotional content or social context — because the mechanism is proprioceptive confirmation, not emotional regulation. ND individuals with documented proprioceptive signal quality deficits should show window collapse under cognitive load even when respiratory mechanics are intact — because Link 3 is an independent anchoring pathway. **New invariant — Anchoring style sets the effective prediction‑window ceiling:**
The anchoring substrate does not only determine whether the prediction window 
can open — it determines the ceiling that bounds how far it can open. 
Diaphragmatic, CO₂‑tolerant anchoring allows the full oscillatory amplitude 
the architecture is capable of, producing genuine wide‑window cognitive range. 
Chest‑dominant, bracing‑based anchoring creates a false ceiling: the system 
aborts the pressure cycle before completion, not because structural capacity is 
absent but because the chemoreceptor calibration treats full‑exhale CO₂ levels 
as a threat signal. The diaphragm may be capable of full excursion. The system 
does not permit it.

This distinguishes two mechanistically different failure modes that produce 
identical cognitive outputs: structural ceiling failure (diaphragm or thoracic 
compliance genuinely degraded) and false‑ceiling failure (diaphragm capable but 
bracing onset fires before the pressure cycle completes). Structural ceiling 
failure is visible on spirometry. False‑ceiling failure is invisible to 
spirometry but visible in HRV amplitude during naturalistic activity versus 
controlled breathing tasks — the gap between the two is the amplitude utilization 
fraction. The distinction matters because the interventions differ: diaphragmatic 
strengthening and thoracic mobility work address the structural ceiling; CO₂ 
tolerance training and bracing interruption address the false ceiling. Routing 
the wrong intervention produces limited results for mechanistically correct 
reasons.

## Invariant — False Ceilings and Half‑Capacity Operation

When the autonomic system cannot follow full oscillatory amplitude — through diaphragm underuse, chest‑dominant breathing, or early bracing onset — the anchoring system creates false ceilings rather than allowing the system to destabilize. The false ceiling is a protective mechanism. It prevents the oscillation from moving into ranges where the autonomic system cannot maintain phase coherence. The cost is that the cognitive layer inherits the ceiling and operates at reduced capacity while experiencing no direct signal that the ceiling is artificial.

False ceilings are distinguished from structural ceilings by their context‑dependence and reversibility. A structural ceiling is uniform across states — a degraded diaphragm cannot produce more pressure regardless of cognitive load or sympathetic tone. A false ceiling is load‑dependent and ascending: higher sympathetic tone lowers the CO₂ bracing threshold, which moves the 
bracing onset earlier in the exhale, which reduces the amplitude actually completed, which narrows the effective ceiling further. The person may demonstrate full diaphragmatic range in a calm clinical assessment and reassert a false ceiling within moments of re‑entering load. This is why breathing capacity assessments under calm conditions systematically overestimate the operating ceiling that determines actual cognitive range.

The prior calibration consequence of chronic false‑ceiling operation is not only reduced processing capacity — it is systematic prior bias. Priors are calibrated against inputs sampled across the oscillatory cycle. A system that chronically operates in the narrow phase of the oscillation samples more inputs under narrow‑window conditions. The prior becomes calibrated toward the world‑as‑perceived‑while‑narrow — more threat‑salient, more binary, more pattern‑locked. This is not a processing bias applied to individual inputs. It is a population‑level calibration error: the prior reflects a distribution of sampled experience that was systematically collected under constrained conditions. Interventions that widen the window in the short term correct processing. Only sustained wide‑window operation recalibrates the prior.

Diaphragmatic, CO₂‑mediated anchoring is the corrective architecture. The "floaty" quality reported in full diaphragmatic breathing states — the sense of expanded range and reduced reactivity — is the phenomenological correlate of the system operating at its designed amplitude for the first time rather than within the false ceiling. The system does not feel calmer because tension has been removed. It feels different because the oscillation is reaching phases it was previously aborting before completion.

**Chain completeness:** The CO₂ bracing threshold as exhale abort mechanism is load‑bearing in respiratory physiology. The false‑ceiling concept as a functional limit distinct from structural capacity is scaffolding — mechanistically complete, requiring direct empirical assembly through comparison of spirometry versus naturalistic HRV amplitude in matched populations. The 
prior‑calibration consequence of chronic narrow‑phase operation is scaffolding derived from the prediction‑window architecture, with indirect support from negative interpretive bias literature. Amplitude utilization fraction as a clinical measure distinct from peak capacity is a framework‑derived prediction awaiting instrumentation.

## Invariant — The Threat-Calibration Loop

The false ceiling mechanism does not merely cap cognitive range. Under sustained operation it generates a self-reinforcing feedback loop that progressively recalibrates the prior toward threat, which then tightens the false ceiling further. The loop runs as follows:

Amplitude restriction produces a narrow prediction window. A narrow window spends more time in the exhale-gate phase — the precision-locked, threat-prioritizing state. The world sampled from this phase is sharper, more binary, more danger-weighted than the world the full oscillation samples. The prior updates toward that distribution. Threat-biased priors increase sympathetic tone. Elevated sympathetic tone lowers the CO₂ bracing threshold, moving bracing onset earlier in the exhale. Earlier bracing onset reduces the amplitude actually completed. Reduced amplitude narrows the window further. The loop closes.

This is not a cognitive process. It does not require catastrophic thinking, avoidance behavior, or conscious threat appraisal. It runs entirely on mechanical substrate: diaphragm mechanics, CO₂ chemoreceptor threshold, oscillation amplitude, and the sampling distribution that amplitude determines. The prior drift toward threat is an arithmetic consequence of a narrowed 
sampling distribution, not a psychological response to experience.

The loop is evolutionarily well-motivated. Amplitude restriction under genuine threat is adaptive — it reduces degrees of freedom, collapses ambiguity, accelerates response, and eliminates the sampling-mode delay that deliberation requires. A system that narrows its window when threatened, acts fast, and remains narrowed until the threat resolves is doing exactly what survival 
selection shaped it to do. The problem is specificity of trigger. Modern environments produce the sympathetic tone, postural bracing, and CO₂ sensitivity that fire the loop without the actual threat context that would make the narrowing adaptive. The loop runs as designed. The threat it is responding to is not present.

The loop is also the mechanism behind the population-level drift toward threat-salient priors across adulthood described in the INTERO↔DEV failure modes. It is not a single event. It is a slow, continuous, mechanically-driven recalibration that compounds across years of reduced amplitude utilization. The prior does not drift because of accumulated bad experiences. It drifts because the sampling distribution it is calibrated against has been progressively narrowed by false-ceiling calcification, and the world sampled from the narrow phase is systematically more threat-weighted than the world sampled from the full oscillation.

**Interrupting the loop requires bottom-layer intervention.** Because the loop is driven by amplitude restriction, CO₂ bracing threshold, and proprioceptive looseness — not by beliefs, narratives, identity, or values — interventions that target the cognitive layer cannot interrupt it. Cognitive reframing does not change the CO₂ bracing threshold. Narrative restructuring does not widen 
the exhale amplitude. The loop is mechanical before it is cognitive. Amplitude restoration — through CO₂ tolerance training, diaphragmatic anchoring, and bracing interruption — is the primary lever. When amplitude is restored, sympathetic tone decreases, the bracing threshold rises, the sampling distribution widens, and the prior begins recalibrating toward the less 
threat-weighted world the full oscillation reveals. The phenomenological correlate — the sense of coming back online, of expanded range, of reduced reactivity — is the prior beginning to sample from a wider distribution for the first time in the loop's operating period.

The loop explains the failure of mindset interventions, the mechanism behind cognitive rigidity accumulating with age, the reason some people never recover nuance after high-load periods, and why breathing works when argument does not. It is one circuit. It runs in one direction without intervention. It is interruptible only from below.

**Chain completeness:** The individual links of the loop are each established across upstream contracts — amplitude restriction in ANCHOR↔COG, sympathetic tone → CO₂ bracing threshold interaction in AUTO↔MOD, prior calibration drift from narrow-phase sampling in the False Ceilings invariant above. The complete circuit as a self-reinforcing attractor is a cross-contract derivation: scaffolding, mechanistically complete, requiring longitudinal measurement of oscillation amplitude alongside prior calibration drift to confirm the directional sequence as a closed loop rather than correlated variables with a shared upstream cause.
