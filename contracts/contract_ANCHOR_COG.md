# Contract: ANCHOR ↔ COG

### _Mechanical Anchoring of Prediction Windows_

---

## Contract Summary

**Input layer (ANCHOR):**

- Diaphragmatic excursion amplitude
- Exhale completeness
- Intrathoracic pressure differential
- Bilateral rhythmic proprioceptive confirmation
- Postural symmetry / hemidiaphragm balance
- Vestibular stabilization
- Interoceptive signal clarity (cardiac, respiratory, gastric)

**Output layer (COG):**

- Prediction window width
- Temporal projection range
- Prior weighting ratio
- Precision gain stability
- Model-of-other depth
- Cognitive thread capacity
- Resistance to runaway priors

**Primary crossover point:** Pressure-anchored prediction window stability — the mechanical signal that determines whether the cognitive layer can hold a wide window or collapses into exhale-gate dominance.

This contract unifies a mechanism that appears across multiple contracts in fragmented form: incomplete exhale compressing oscillation amplitude (gap_analysis.md), postural asymmetry producing hemidiaphragm imbalance and RSA asymmetry (gap_analysis.json), bilateral rhythmic movement entraining the prediction cycle (PROPRIO↔AUTO/COG), and interoceptive contact preventing runaway priors (minimal_core.md). These are all the same mechanism. This contract names it.

## Mechanistic Chain

### Link 1 — Mechanical anchor → RSA amplitude

Diaphragm excursion depth, exhale completeness, and thoracic symmetry jointly determine RSA amplitude, HRV bandwidth, and vagal afferent signal quality. Breathing is HRV at the mechanical level — the pressure differential generated per breath cycle is the direct physical driver of the oscillation that RSA measures. A strong anchor produces a strong oscillation. A weak anchor compresses the oscillation amplitude before any downstream cognitive effect is possible. This is the entry point of the chain.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing. Established across respiratory physiology and HRV literature.

---

### Link 2 — RSA amplitude → prediction window width

The oscillation range determines how far the prediction window can open on inhale. Inhale opens the prediction window; exhale narrows it. This is the breath-gated cognitive cycle described in COG↔REASON. The critical addition this contract makes is that the *range* of the oscillation is mechanically constrained upstream. If the oscillation amplitude is compressed — through incomplete exhale, shallow breathing, or asymmetric diaphragm loading — the window cannot widen fully on inhale regardless of cognitive intent. The mechanical ceiling is set before the cognitive layer acts.

This is the mechanical origin of tunnel vision, recursive thinking, rigid priors, loss of curiosity, and binary reasoning under load. These are not primarily cognitive failures. They are outputs of a mechanically compressed oscillation.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing via RSA-cognition literature; the specific amplitude-ceiling mechanism is scaffolding — mechanistically coherent, direct measurement pending.

---

### Link 3 — Proprioceptive confirmation → precision gain stability

Bilateral rhythmic proprioceptive input provides a second anchoring signal operating in parallel with respiratory mechanics. Clean proprioceptive error signals entrain the prediction cycle, generating rhythmic cerebellar prediction-confirmation streams that stabilize precision gain. When proprioception is degraded — through mechanoreceptor signal noise, connective tissue laxity, or motor forward model uncertainty — prediction error becomes noisy, sympathetic load rises, precision gain spikes, and the window narrows independently of respiratory mechanics. This is the mechanism behind ND motor-planning patterns and load-dependent cognitive switching in populations with proprioceptive signal quality deficits.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Mechanistically complete via PROPRIO↔AUTO/COG. Requires direct measurement of precision gain modulation by proprioceptive signal quality.

---

### Link 4 — Interoceptive clarity → prior correctability

Interoceptive signal clarity is the third anchoring input. Prediction error from ascending interoceptive signals is what keeps priors correctable — the body-state signal that allows the generative model to update against incoming evidence rather than run forward on prior momentum. When interoception is degraded, priors become increasingly uncorrectable. The system cannot distinguish a prior-generated signal from a real ascending signal. Runaway narrative chains emerge. Fallacy groups 5 and 6 from COG↔REASON activate — interlocutor models simplify, confabulation increases, and the prior drives behavior rather than evidence.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing via COG↔INTERO. The specific interoceptive-clarity-to-prior-correctability link is confirmed in chronic pain and FND literature.

---

### Link 5 — Anchor failure → exhale-gate dominance

When all three anchoring inputs degrade simultaneously — incomplete exhale, proprioceptive noise, interoceptive degradation — the system enters exhale-gate dominance. The prediction window is frozen at its narrowest point. The oscillation that would normally allow sampling-mode reopening on inhale is absent because the mechanical substrate for it has collapsed. The cognitive layer experiences this as the inability to think clearly, change perspective, or update beliefs — but the cause is upstream of cognition entirely. Attributing this state to stress, emotion, or cognitive failure misidentifies the mechanism and directs intervention to the wrong layer.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Mechanistically complete. Prospective measurement of oscillation amplitude against reasoning flexibility required for direct confirmation.

## Failure Modes

**Misattribution of mechanical collapse to cognitive cause:**
Anchor failure produces a cognitive output — narrowed window, rigid priors, binary reasoning — that is phenomenologically indistinguishable from a cognitively-generated state. The person experiencing it and the clinician observing it both reach for cognitive explanations. The intervention that follows — cognitive restructuring, emotional processing, top-down reappraisal — targets the wrong layer. The mechanical substrate remains uncorrected and the cognitive intervention produces limited results, which then gets attributed to treatment resistance or insufficient motivation.

**External anchor dependency:**
Social co-regulation, therapist presence, and secure attachment all function as external anchors — they borrow prediction window width from another regulated nervous system and temporarily widen the local window. This is the ESLPM mechanism. The failure mode is when external anchoring substitutes for internal anchor development rather than scaffolding it. The window widens during the co-regulated interaction and collapses when the external anchor is removed. Dependency emerges not from attachment pathology but from an unrepaired internal anchor deficit that external regulation cannot permanently correct.

**Asymmetric diaphragm loading producing lateralized bias:**
Postural asymmetry that loads one hemidiaphragm more than the other produces asymmetric RSA and asymmetric hemispheric activation. The prediction window opens unevenly across hemispheres. Reasoning becomes laterally biased in ways that track the postural asymmetry rather than the cognitive content of the task. This is not a reasoning error at the cognitive level — it is a physical asymmetry in the pressure mechanics producing a systematic bias in which hemisphere drives the generative model at any given moment.

**Mindfulness without anchor repair:**
Mindfulness practice reduces prior precision to allow updating from incoming signals. When the anchor is degraded — respiratory mechanics compressed, proprioception noisy, interoception unclear — the signals available to update the prior are low quality. Opening precision weighting onto degraded signals does not produce accurate prior recalibration. It produces either amplified noise or continued prior dominance. Mindfulness without upstream anchor repair produces limited results for the same mechanistic reason as in COG↔INTERO: the signal being attended to is not clean enough to update the prior accurately.

## Operating States

| State | Anchor Quality | Prediction Window | Cognitive Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stable anchor** | Strong diaphragm, full exhale, bilateral proprioceptive confirmation | Wide, oscillating | High reasoning capacity, multi-threading, curiosity |
| **Weak anchor** | Shallow exhale, postural asymmetry, reduced proprioception | Narrowed | Binary reasoning, short temporal horizon, reduced model-of-other depth |
| **Anchor collapse** | Incomplete exhale + proprioceptive noise + interoceptive degradation | Frozen at exhale-gate | Rigid priors, runaway chains, fallacy groups 5–6 active |
| **External anchor** | Social co-regulation, therapist, regulated partner | Borrowed width | Window widens temporarily; collapses on removal if internal anchor unrepaired |

## Predictions This Contract Generates

**Prediction 1 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Exhale completeness should correlate with prediction window width independent of breathing rate — because the oscillation amplitude, not the oscillation frequency, sets the cognitive ceiling. A slow shallow breath and a fast shallow breath should produce equivalent window narrowing; a slow complete breath should produce the widest window regardless of rate.

**Prediction 2 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
A single deliberate full exhale should produce measurable widening of prediction window performance on cognitive flexibility tasks more rapidly than any equivalent-duration cognitive intervention. The effect should be observable within one breath cycle. This is the most testable prediction the contract generates and the most diagnostic for the mechanical-vs-cognitive account.

**Prediction 3 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Asymmetric diaphragm loading — induced experimentally through sustained lateral posture — should produce measurable lateralized reasoning bias that correlates with the degree of postural asymmetry and resolves on symmetry restoration. This prediction separates the mechanical account from any cognitive or emotional account of reasoning asymmetry.

**Prediction 4 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Bilateral rhythmic movement should restore precision gain stability and widen prediction windows even in the complete absence of emotional content or social context — because the mechanism is proprioceptive confirmation, not emotional regulation. Effect size should be equivalent across emotionally neutral and emotionally loaded contexts.

**Prediction 5 — Chain completeness: Predictive**
ND individuals with documented proprioceptive signal quality deficits should show window collapse under cognitive load even when respiratory mechanics are intact — because Link 3 is an independent anchoring pathway. Collapse should correlate with proprioceptive noise measures, not with breathing pattern measures, isolating the proprioceptive contribution.

**Prediction 6 — Chain completeness: Predictive**
Interoceptive accuracy training should increase prior correctability and reduce runaway reasoning chains independent of any change in cognitive strategy — because the mechanism is signal quality improvement, not metacognitive instruction. Effect should be measurable in tasks designed to elicit prior-dominance errors, with improvement tracking interoceptive accuracy gains rather than self-reported strategy change.
