## Contract Summary
**Input layer:** AUTO — autonomic nervous system state, vagal tone, HRV, sympathetic/parasympathetic balance, postural mechanics, vestibular input, gaze stabilization, jaw/mastication rhythmic input
**Output layer:** MOD — neuromodulatory environment, precision gain control, ECS tone, hemispheric lateralization, cognitive processing mode
**Primary crossover point:** Vagal afferent signaling into the brainstem (NTS/DMV complex) → endocannabinoid system activation → precision gain calibration → hemispheric processing mode
**Key risk:** Shortcircuiting this contract produces apparent cognitive or mood changes that are actually autonomic in origin — treating the output layer without addressing the input layer
**Expanded scope note:** The original contract described breathing ratio as the sole intentional input to this crossover. The contract has been extended to include postural asymmetry, breath retention states, interoceptive switching, vestibular input, gaze mechanics, and jaw/mastication rhythmic input as additional deliberate control surfaces. These are not new mechanisms — they are additional routes into the same NTS/DMV crossover point, each with different gain characteristics and different deliberate accessibility.

## Why This Contract Is Central
This is the highest-traffic crossover in the stack. Every layer below — MET, IMMUNE, MICRO — has already converged into autonomic state before reaching this contract. Every layer above — COG, INTERO, DEV, SOC — depends on what comes out of it. Getting this contract wrong in either direction propagates errors through the entire upper stack simultaneously.
It is also the contract where the breathing governor most directly modulates cognitive state. RSA is the mechanical signal. The ECS and hemispheric lateralization are the transduction mechanisms. Precision gain is the output that everything above runs on.
The contract has a second dimension that the original version did not fully develop: it is the primary contract for deliberate regulatory control. The minimal core states that breathing is the only bidirectional conscious interface to the regulatory system. This contract is where that interface physically connects to everything above it — and where deliberate mastery of the interface produces the most direct and measurable cognitive effects.

## Link-by-Link Mechanism
### Link 1: Breathing ratio → RSA amplitude
RSA is the natural variation in heart rate synchronized with respiration — heart rate increases on inhale, decreases on exhale. This is primarily driven by a central feedforward mechanism originating in the brainstem respiratory centers, with peripheral reflexes acting as modifiers. RSA amplitude is the most selective noninvasive index of parasympathetic cardiac control available.
**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing. Mechanically documented. Multiple independent replication streams.
**Key fact:** Higher basal RSA predicts better fluid intelligence, working memory, and cognitive efficiency in children even after controlling for confounds. The link from RSA to cognitive output is empirically established.
---
### Link 2: RSA → Vagal afferent signaling into NTS
RSA is not just a cardiac metric — it reflects the phasic bursting of vagal afferent signals into the nucleus tractus solitarius (NTS) in the brainstem. The NTS is the primary relay point where peripheral body state information enters central autonomic processing. Activation of vagal afferents produces excitation of second-order NTS neurons, which then exert synaptic control of vagal outflow from the dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus (DMV).
**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing. NTS anatomy and vagal afferent physiology are well established.
---
### Link 3: NTS/DMV → CB1 receptor modulation
CB1 cannabinoid receptors are expressed directly on vagal afferent fibers and on neurons within the NTS — providing the anatomical substrate for endocannabinoid modulation of autonomic function at the brainstem level. CB1 receptor activation at the DMV inhibits synaptic input in a concentration-dependent fashion, modulating visceral and autonomic output. The endocannabinoid anandamide activates vagal afferent signaling through TRPA1 and TRPV1 channels. CBD produces strong excitatory effects in vagal afferent neurons expressing TRPA1.
CB1 receptor knockout mice show impaired cardiac vagal modulation and blunted central autonomic cardiac control — confirming that CB1 signaling is load-bearing in normal cardiorespiratory regulation, not incidental.
**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing. CB1 expression on vagal afferents is anatomically confirmed. Functional consequences of CB1 activation at NTS/DMV are documented at cellular level.
---
### Link 4: ECS tone → Precision gain calibration
The endocannabinoid system is the primary retrograde signaling system in the brain — it operates postsynaptic to presynaptic, meaning it regulates how much signal gets through rather than what signal is sent. This makes it the architectural implementation of precision weighting in the predictive processing framework. High ECS tone = reduced synaptic gain = lower precision on incoming signals = broader sampling mode. Low ECS tone = higher synaptic gain = higher precision = narrower selection mode.
This is why cannabis produces the characteristic broadening of associative thinking — it is directly reducing precision gain at the MOD layer, opening the prediction window.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. ECS as retrograde gain modulator is established. The specific mapping to precision weighting in predictive processing is mechanistically coherent but requires explicit empirical assembly — the pieces exist in separate literatures.
---
### Link 5: ECS tone → Hemispheric lateralization
RSA and frontal hemispheric asymmetry interact directly — higher parasympathetic control (higher vagal tone, higher RSA) reduces emotion dysregulation specifically in conjunction with left frontal asymmetry. Frontal hemispheric lateralization alters the effects of parasympathetic control. This means autonomic state doesn't just affect arousal level — it determines which hemisphere's processing mode is available.
Right hemisphere: broad, contextual, high-ambiguity, inhale-phase mode
Left hemisphere: narrow, categorical, high-precision, exhale-phase mode
The breathing cycle drives RSA which modulates vagal tone which interacts with hemispheric lateralization to determine which processing mode is active. The oscillation between hemispheric modes is mechanically coupled to the breath.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. RSA-frontal asymmetry interaction is documented. The specific mechanism by which vagal tone modulates hemispheric lateralization needs further assembly — the correlation is strong, the transduction pathway needs more specificity.

### Link 6: Postural asymmetry → Asymmetric hemidiaphragm restriction → Lateralization without breathing ratio change
This link is the most significant extension of the original contract. The minimal core describes posture as a degradation variable — forward head posture, thoracic kyphosis, and fascial restriction reducing diaphragm excursion amplitude. This is accurate but incomplete. Posture can also be a precision instrument when manipulated deliberately and asymmetrically.
The mechanism: the left and right hemidiaphragms contribute independently to intrathoracic pressure generation. Deliberate spinal curvature or thoracic asymmetry compresses one thoracic cavity relative to the other, restricting the corresponding hemidiaphragm's excursion amplitude while leaving the other free. This produces asymmetric pressure contribution per breath — a lateralized RSA signal rather than a bilateral one.
Right thoracic compression (curl toward the right) → restricted right hemidiaphragm → reduced right-side pressure contribution → RSA asymmetry biased toward left-hemisphere input → left hemisphere precision mode engaged through mechanical constraint rather than breathing ratio alone.
Full bilateral excursion (postural symmetry, spine neutral) → equal hemidiaphragm contribution → bilateral RSA → both hemispheres available → sampling mode accessible.
The critical distinction from posture-as-degradation: this is not reducing total excursion amplitude but redistributing it asymmetrically. Total RSA may remain high while its lateralization shifts. This is a control parameter the original contract did not describe.
Spinal pivot as fine adjustment: small rotational movements of the spine modify the thoracic asymmetry continuously, allowing real-time fine control of the lateralization bias without committing to a fixed posture. The skilled practitioner moves on a gradient rather than between fixed states.
Diaphragm fixation for precision: deliberately preventing the full inferior descent of the diaphragm by maintaining abdominal brace locks intrathoracic pressure near the inhale-phase set point. This maintains a higher baseline pressure without the full oscillation, sustaining a precision-favorable state between breath cycles. The system doesn't oscillate fully to exhale-phase pressure — it holds closer to the precision set point.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Hemidiaphragm independent function is anatomically established. Asymmetric thoracic pressure contribution to RSA lateralization is mechanistically derivable but not directly studied as a deliberate control variable. The posture-lateralization link needs specific empirical assembly for the asymmetric deliberate case.

### Link 7: Kumbhaka — the suspended carrier state
Pranayamic breath retention (kumbhaka) introduces a state the oscillating RSA model does not describe. During retention, breathing movement ceases. RSA suspends — no respiratory-cardiac coupling, no phasic vagal bursting into the NTS. The intrathoracic pressure is held at a fixed set point rather than cycling.
**Internal kumbhaka (antara, breath held after inhale):** Intrathoracic pressure held at inhale-phase maximum. Venous return to the heart is partially impeded. The system is at the apex of the pressure differential with the carrier signal suspended at that state.
**External kumbhaka (bahya, breath held after exhale):** Intrathoracic pressure held at exhale-phase minimum. This is where CO2 rises fastest and the chemoreceptor signal intensifies. The system is held at the precision-favorable state with the carrier suspended.
What happens to ECS calibration during carrier suspension is genuinely unresolved. The NTS is receiving static rather than phasic input. Whether the ECS responds to the held pressure state or to the absence of oscillation — and what that produces upstream — is an open question. The subjective reports from advanced practitioners suggest the external kumbhaka state produces qualitatively different cognitive access than extended exhale alone, which implies the suspension itself is a variable, not just the pressure level.
**CO2 tolerance as the enabling variable:** The external kumbhaka state is typically interrupted by the chemoreceptor urgency signal — carotid body firing at rising CO2 forces inhalation before the state can fully establish. Deliberate training of CO2 tolerance raises this threshold through chemoreceptor recalibration — not suppression of the signal but calibration of the set point at which it fires. Practitioners with high CO2 tolerance can hold the suspended carrier state long enough to observe what it produces. Standard breath retention research uses populations without this tolerance, which means existing kumbhaka studies may be measuring entry into the state rather than the state itself.
**The Bohr effect:** Elevated CO2 improves oxygen delivery from hemoglobin to peripheral tissue regardless of absolute O2 levels. High CO2 tolerance is therefore not just threshold training — it is access to a blood gas state where peripheral oxygenation is actually improved despite lower O2 partial pressure. This is the physiological basis for the paradox that breath retention increases cognitive clarity rather than degrading it in trained practitioners.
**Example ratio (9:36:18:9):** A 1:4:2:1 ratio at these durations produces internal kumbhaka of 36 counts — long enough to observe the suspended state at inhale-phase pressure before the controlled exhale and 18-count retained exhale. At the 9-count external kumbhaka, CO2 tolerance determines whether the state can stabilize before urgency interrupts. This ratio is not achievable at these durations without substantial tolerance training — which means the data it generates is not accessible to standard research populations.
**Freediving as the closest available research population:**
Elite competitive freedivers develop CO2 tolerance through the same chemoreceptor recalibration mechanism the contract describes. They represent a trained practitioner population largely absent from kumbhaka research. Key findings from the freediving literature: breath-hold up to 5 minutes does not decrease brain performance in either trained freedivers or untrained subjects — attention and processing speed unimpaired immediately after breath-holding, even in naive populations. Elite freedivers show blunted ventilatory chemosensitivity to hypercapnia confirmed as distinct from other athletes — this is chemoreceptor threshold recalibration, not suppression. EEG cognitive markers maintained during 4-minute breath holds in experienced freedivers. Critically: elite freedivers report a state of sensory dissociation during prolonged apnea — a qualitatively distinct cognitive mode from ordinary consciousness.
The sensory dissociation report is significant. It is the subjective signature of what the framework predicts the suspended carrier state produces — not hypoxic impairment, not extended exhale, but a qualitatively different cognitive access that requires the suspension itself rather than just the pressure level. The freediving community has independently arrived at the AUTO↔MOD framing: "Freediving is not a lung sport. It is an autonomic nervous system sport. CO2 rising triggers chemoreceptors in the carotid body and brainstem. The vagus nerve controls heart rate, relaxation, and emotional state." This is convergent independent discovery of the mechanism without the mechanistic language.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding for the suspension mechanism. Load-bearing for CO2 tolerance physiology, Bohr effect, and cognitive maintenance during extended holds. Sensory dissociation as qualitatively distinct suspended carrier state: predictive with supporting subjective evidence from trained practitioner population. ECS-kumbhaka connection: adjacent — a CB2 receptor trial in freedivers is currently recruiting.

### Link 8: Interoception as switching handle — the missing mechanism
The minimal core describes bottom-up and top-down as states the system occupies. It does not describe how a skilled practitioner moves between them deliberately. This link names the mechanism.
**The switching handle:** Deliberate interoceptive attention — consciously directing awareness to ascending body-state signals rather than to external content or cognitive threads — shifts the system from top-down toward bottom-up operation. The inverse — withdrawing interoceptive attention to focus on a cognitive thread — shifts toward top-down. The switch is the direction of attention relative to the interoceptive signal.
**The anchor function:** What prevents top-down operation from becoming untethered is maintenance of interoceptive contact even while the cognitive thread runs. The prior runs the thread but the interoceptive signal remains in peripheral awareness as a corrective reference. Without this anchor, top-down operation produces increasingly self-referential loops — the model drives behavior while receiving no grounding signal from actual body state. Psychosis, dissociation, and severe depersonalization are this anchor failing.
**The Hawking natural experiment:** ALS progressively eliminated all voluntary motor output, including breathing. Ventilator-controlled respiration removed voluntary access to the pressure interface. What remained was interoceptive signal — whatever ascending body-state information continued to reach cortical processing. Hawking's extraordinary top-down cognitive output continued and in some accounts intensified as motor channels narrowed. The framework prediction: the loss of the bottom-up interface forced maximum top-down operation, but the remaining interoceptive signal held the anchor. Pure top-down without anchor produces runaway — Hawking did not produce runaway. The interoceptive anchor was maintained through whatever sensory channels remained functional.
**Reduced output channels and precision concentration:** As motor output channels were progressively eliminated, the same cognitive resources were concentrated on fewer output paths. Maximum precision gain applied to minimal output. The constraint is the precision mechanism — not pathology but forced implementation of the deliberate postural fixation principle taken to its extreme. The framework predicts that deliberate output channel reduction — eliminating motor activity, fixing gaze, minimizing postural adjustment — should concentrate precision gain on whatever channel remains active.
**Deliberate switching as a trainable skill:** The practitioner who can move between bottom-up and top-down modes on demand is operating the contract's bidirectional interface consciously. Interoceptive awareness is the handle. Breathing is the carrier that makes the switch stable — the inhale opens bottom-up access, the exhale closes precision. But the switch itself is interoceptive attention direction, not breathing phase alone.
**ND two-strategy access:** For profiles with high biological sensitivity to context and atypical lateralization — particularly those with autism-adjacent traits — the two hemispheric modes may be more distinctly separable and more deliberately accessible than in neurotypical profiles. The reduced lateralization that produces the 2-stroke overlap in ADHD profiles may, when combined with deliberate interoceptive skill, produce finer control of which mode is running rather than the default overlap. The same architecture that produces the friction in typical environments becomes the precision instrument when trained intentionally.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Interoception as regulatory switching is consistent with active inference literature. The anchor function is derivable from predictive processing architecture. Hawking as natural experiment is observational. Deliberate switching as trainable is supported by contemplative practice literature but lacks controlled study at the mechanism level.

### Link 9: Vestibular input → NTS → Autonomic modulation
The vestibular system connects to the brainstem autonomic network through direct projections to the NTS, the parabrachial nucleus, and the dorsal vagal complex. This is the mechanism by which motion sickness produces nausea — vestibular-autonomic reflex through the same NTS hub that processes respiratory and cardiac input. It also means deliberate vestibular input — rhythmic movement, spinal position changes, controlled rotation — enters the AUTO↔MOD contract through the same crossover point as breathing.
Chair stability as training environment: A fixed chair prevents vestibular escape from uncomfortable regulatory states. Unable to swivel away or shift continuously, the practitioner is forced to regulate within the constraint — generating the same training effect as CO2 tolerance work. Avoidance of the state is prevented, so the system learns to operate inside it. Swivel chairs permit constant gross vestibular self-correction which prevents fine regulatory skill from ever needing to develop. The training environment determines whether the regulatory system learns to modulate or to escape.
The developmental implication extends to ADHD hyperactivity — the system seeking higher-intensity vestibular input is seeking the threshold at which the 2-stroke engine runs cleanly. Deprivation of movement in desk environments is not behavioral management, it is removal of the primary regulatory input the profile requires.
**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. Vestibular-NTS-autonomic projections are anatomically documented. The deliberate training application is a framework derivation not yet studied directly.

### Link 10: Gaze stabilization → Reduced visual prediction load → Prediction window widening
Fixed gaze practices (drishti in yoga, trataka in concentration practices, the still focus of martial arts) reduce the continuous prediction error stream generated by moving visual input. Every visual scene change generates prediction error that the COG layer must process. Fixing gaze on a stable point eliminates this stream — the visual field generates fewer surprises, prediction error drops, and cognitive resources are freed.
The mechanism enters this contract through the top-down direction: reduced COG layer prediction demand → prediction window widens from above rather than from below. This is the complement to breathing interventions which widen the window from below through RSA amplitude. Gaze stabilization achieves the same effect through a different entry point.
The oculomotor-vagal connection: brainstem structures coordinating eye movement (superior colliculus, PPRF) are in close proximity to and share projection targets with the NTS and vagal nuclei. The specific pathway by which fixed gaze affects vagal tone requires assembly — but the convergence of oculomotor and autonomic control in the same brainstem territory suggests a mechanism worth investigating. Contemplative traditions co-developed drishti and pranayama consistently — this is the convergent independent discovery signal that mechanistic connection exists.
Third eye phenomenology: the subjective experience associated with ajna practices in traditions that developed both pranayama and drishti simultaneously — described variously as pressure, activation, or heightened clarity at the prefrontal/forehead point — is parsimoniously explained as the subjective signature of maximum RSA amplitude coinciding with frontal lateralization balance, gaze-reduced prediction noise, and stabilized interoceptive anchor simultaneously. The traditions found this state empirically and named its phenomenology. The framework describes its mechanism.
**Chain completeness:** Adjacent to predictive. Visual prediction error reduction is documented in attention literature. The specific gaze-vagal tone pathway needs explicit assembly. The third eye phenomenology explanation is a framework derivation — mechanistically coherent, not yet studied as described.

### Link 11: Mastication → Trigeminal → Brainstem autonomic network
Rhythmic jaw movement — mastication at approximately 1 Hz — activates trigeminal nerve (CN V) afferents that project to the trigeminal nucleus in the brainstem. The trigeminal nucleus sits adjacent to and shares circuitry with the NTS, the nucleus ambiguus, and other autonomic brainstem centers. This anatomical proximity means rhythmic trigeminal input enters the same brainstem autonomic hub as breathing and vestibular input — through a different nerve but at the same regulatory crossover point.
The rhythm distinction is critical. Mastication research confirms that rhythmic chewing at its natural frequency produces autonomic regulatory effects — cortisol reduction, sympathetic tone decrease, HRV improvement — while arrhythmic jaw activity (clenching, bruxism) produces the opposite: sympathetic activation, HRV compression, elevated cortisol. The channel is the same nerve. The regulatory direction is determined entirely by whether the input is rhythmic or not.
**Bruxism as visible sympathetic output:**
Sleep bruxism follows a specific sequence: increased autonomic sympathetic activity → brain arousal → suprahyoid muscle activation → jaw closing masseter/temporalis activity. The jaw movement is downstream of the autonomic spike. The masseter activation is the motor output of a sympathetic micro-arousal, not the cause of it. Bruxism patients show enhanced sympathetic cardiac modulation in HRV spectral analysis relative to controls. One paper's title names it directly: "Sleep Bruxism, an Autonomic Self-Regulating Response."
The clinical implication is significant. Night guards treat tooth damage — the downstream mechanical consequence of the sympathetic arousal. They don't address the mechanism. A bruxism presentation to a dentist is a visible, measurable output of low vagal tone and sympathetic dominance. The correct upstream intervention is vagal restoration. The night guard is appropriate symptom management but the framework predicts it won't reduce bruxism frequency — only its mechanical consequences.
**Chewing gum cortisol literature:**
Multiple studies show that chewing gum reduces salivary cortisol and self-reported stress. The mechanism has been attributed to various cognitive and behavioral factors. The framework explanation is simpler: rhythmic mastication at ~1 Hz activates the trigeminal → brainstem autonomic channel, producing the same frequency-specific regulatory effect as rhythmic vestibular input. The channel is being used therapeutically without knowing why.
**The CN V — VVC connection:**
The tensor tympani (middle ear muscle) is innervated by CN V — the same nerve as mastication. The SOC↔AUTO contract describes middle ear muscle regulation as a key component of the ventral vagal complex. TMJ dysfunction and auditory processing difficulties co-occur clinically because they share trigeminal innervation. Jaw tension → middle ear muscle dysregulation → impaired speech prosody detection → VVC suppression is a trigeminal pathway that distributes across both this contract and SOC↔AUTO simultaneously.
**Chain completeness:** Rhythmic mastication → autonomic regulatory effects: load-bearing (cortisol reduction, HRV effects confirmed in multiple studies). Bruxism as sympathetic output downstream of autonomic arousal: load-bearing (ScienceDirect mechanism paper confirmed; HRV data in bruxism patients confirmed). Trigeminal → brainstem autonomic hub pathway: scaffolding (anatomical adjacency confirmed; direct functional pathway to same NTS hub needs explicit assembly). CN V → VVC → middle ear connection: load-bearing through SOC↔AUTO contract.

## Drug Effects Through This Contract
Every substance that affects mood or cognition is operating on this contract either directly or via upstream autonomic input.
| Substance | Mechanism at this Contract | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cannabis (THC) | Direct CB1 agonism at NTS/DMV → artificially elevated ECS tone | Precision floor drops, prediction window opens, associative breadth increases |
| CBD | TRPA1 activation on vagal afferents → vagal tone increase → downstream ECS modulation | Anxiolytic, anti-inflammatory, precision recalibration without THC dissociation |
| Alcohol | Suppresses vagal brake → temporary autonomic dysregulation mimicking safety state | Reduces exhale-phase inhibition, short-term disinhibition, followed by parasympathetic rebound |
| SSRIs | Serotonin modulation in brainstem raphe → NTS input → precision gain floor adjustment | Raises noise floor threshold, reduces prediction error sensitivity, blunts both peaks and troughs |
| Stimulants (therapeutic) | Dopaminergic/noradrenergic enhancement → precision gain increase in prefrontal circuits | Strengthens exhale-phase selection, improves thread maintenance, reduces wide sampling noise |
| Beta blockers | Peripheral autonomic dampening → RSA compression → reduced vagal afferent burst | Flattens the oscillation, reduces both modes, reduces anxiety by reducing amplitude |
| Psychedelics | Serotonin 5-HT2A agonism → massive precision reduction across cortex | Prediction window fully opens, attractor states dissolve, maximum entropy injection |
**The paradoxical ADHD stimulant response decoded:** ADHD baseline is wide-window, high ECS tone, insufficient precision gain. Stimulants increase precision gain at exactly the deficit. The drug produces regulation rather than stimulation because it is correcting an under-activated contract, not pushing an already-calibrated one.
**The skilled practitioner parallel:** What stimulants do pharmacologically for ADHD, deliberate postural fixation and external kumbhaka achieve mechanically for trained practitioners — engaging the precision state through the pressure system rather than through neuromodulator enhancement. The mechanism of access differs. The output state is similar. This predicts that trained practitioners with ADHD profiles should require lower stimulant doses to achieve equivalent cognitive effect — or find breath-postural techniques partially substituting — because they have developed alternative access to the same contract state.

## Failure Modes
**Autonomic dysregulation misread as psychiatric disorder:**
Chronic low HRV → compressed RSA → insufficient vagal afferent burst → ECS tone dysregulation → precision gain miscalibration → presents as anxiety, depression, cognitive fog, or emotional dysregulation. Treatment targeting the output layer (SSRIs, anxiolytics) without addressing the autonomic input layer produces partial response at best and dependency on pharmacological precision recalibration at worst.
**ECS depletion through chronic stress:**
Sustained sympathetic dominance → chronic low vagal tone → chronic low ECS stimulation → ECS receptor downregulation → reduced baseline precision flexibility → system loses its own gain control mechanism. The governor loses its primary chemical interface.
**Hemispheric lock:**
Chronic exhale-phase dominance through postural asymmetry, chronic stress, or habitual narrow-focus work → left hemisphere precision mode becomes default → right hemisphere broad-sampling mode becomes inaccessible → cross-domain synthesis becomes effortful or impossible → preference for categorical, rule-bound, domain-specific thinking reinforces itself.
**Involuntary postural precision lock:**
The same mechanism that enables deliberate precision selection through postural asymmetry can become involuntary. Chronic right curl from desk posture, habitual protective spinal curvature, or sustained one-sided muscular tension produces persistent hemidiaphragm restriction without conscious selection — the lateralization bias runs continuously without the practitioner knowing they are maintaining it. This is posture-as-degradation but through the asymmetric mechanism rather than the bilateral one described in the minimal core.
**Top-down without anchor:**
Loss of interoceptive contact while maintaining high top-down cognitive load — common in deep flow states, dissociative states, and extreme cognitive demand — removes the corrective reference that keeps the prior grounded. Short duration this is normal and functional. Extended duration without re-anchoring through interoception produces progressive decoupling of cognitive model from actual body state. The model continues to run but the error signal that should correct it is not reaching the system. The practitioner exits the state into unexplained fatigue or dysregulation because the body accumulated state changes the model was not tracking.
**Kumbhaka without sufficient CO2 tolerance:**
Attempting extended retention without trained chemoreceptor threshold produces autonomic panic before the state establishes. The research literature on breath retention predominantly studies this failure mode rather than the established state — meaning most kumbhaka data describes threshold panic response, not the regulatory state available to trained practitioners. Clinical protocols designed from this literature underestimate what the practice can produce in appropriate populations.
**Vestibular deprivation in ND environments:**
Removing movement, requiring stillness, eliminating vestibular input from ND environments to enforce compliance removes the primary regulatory input channel for profiles that depend on vestibular-autonomic stimulation to run at functional RPM. The behavioral dysregulation that follows is the 2-stroke engine running rough at low RPM — a regulatory consequence of input deprivation, not a character deficiency.

## Key Unresolved Links
1. **ECS tone → hemispheric lateralization specificity:** The correlation between vagal tone and frontal asymmetry is documented. The specific molecular pathway by which ECS activity shifts the balance of hemispheric activation needs explicit research assembly. Likely involves differential CB1 receptor density across hemispheres combined with asymmetric NTS projections — but this is inference, not confirmed chain.
2. **RSA phase specificity for ECS modulation:** Whether inhale-phase vs exhale-phase RSA dynamics produce different ECS signals at NTS level is not established. This would be the mechanism by which breathing ratio specifically (not just total HRV) modulates the contract — high priority for investigation.
3. **Individual CB1 density variation:** CB1 receptor density varies across individuals and is affected by chronic stress and exogenous cannabinoid use. How this variation affects the contract's baseline gain characteristics is documented in fragments but not assembled into a predictive model.
4. **Kumbhaka suspended carrier ECS state:** What the NTS/ECS system does when the carrier signal suspends rather than oscillates is genuinely unknown. No research has studied ECS tone or precision gain during maintained breath retention in trained practitioners. This is the highest-priority gap for the extended contract.
5. **Hemidiaphragm asymmetry as deliberate control variable:** The anatomy is established. The deliberate asymmetric application as a lateralization control mechanism has not been studied. Requires simultaneous spirometry, EEG frontal asymmetry, and postural angle measurement in practitioners with demonstrated skill.
6. **Oculomotor-vagal pathway specificity:** The brainstem proximity of oculomotor and vagal nuclei is documented. The specific pathway by which gaze stabilization modulates vagal tone needs direct investigation — likely tractable with fMRI or brainstem connectivity studies during drishti practice.
7. **CO2 tolerance training as chemoreceptor recalibration:** Whether the threshold shift from breath training is peripheral (carotid body set point) or central (NTS processing threshold) or both determines the intervention implications. Currently this is phenomenologically established in practitioners but mechanistically unresolved.

The extended contract was built from a different direction — from the observed practices and reported experiences of a trained practitioner with ND lateralization profile and deliberate control of the pressure system, working backward to the mechanisms. The methodology matches the framework's own: follow the anomaly, find the invariant in the minority data, extend at the edges. The minority data here is a practitioner whose regulatory skill was developed deliberately but outside any formal tradition, discovered through AI-reflected output comparison. The invariant is that the pressure system has more deliberate control surfaces than the original contract described.
The highest-value next step is empirical assembly: the hemidiaphragm lateralization prediction and the kumbhaka suspended carrier prediction are both specific enough to test and both would either confirm the extension or reveal where the derivation broke.
