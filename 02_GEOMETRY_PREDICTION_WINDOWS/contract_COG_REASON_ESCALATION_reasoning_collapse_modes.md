## Contract Summary

**Input layer:** ANCHOR — respiratory mechanics, exhale completeness, diaphragmatic excursion, proprioceptive confirmation, interoceptive clarity; AUTO — sympathetic tone, vagal brake state; SOC — identity relevance, social-threat cues; COG — baseline cognitive and emotional load

**Output layer:** REASON — prediction-window width, expansion viability, identity-anchoring threshold, justification-mode activation, reasoning-geometry distortion (curvature increase, geodesic shortening), narrative stabilization

**Primary crossover point:** When pressure-control capacity fails to support the expansion phase that ambiguity requires, sympathetic activation substitutes — narrowing the prediction window, increasing identity salience, and routing reasoning output through narrative coherence rather than evidence coherence. The result is not ideological error. It is threat classification under load, using identity as the cheapest available classifier.

---

### Link 1: Ambiguity requires expansion — the pressure-control dependency

Engaging ambiguity requires the inhale phase of the prediction-window oscillation: a wide sampling space that holds contradictory interpretations simultaneously before committing to one. This expansion phase is mechanically dependent on exhale completeness and diaphragmatic excursion amplitude. The ANCHOR↔COG contract establishes that shallow 
or incomplete exhalation sets a low ceiling on the prediction window, producing tunnel vision and rigid priors regardless of the cognitive content being processed.

The consequence is that pressure-control capacity is a prerequisite for ambiguity tolerance — not a contributing factor but a structural requirement. A person without reliable pressure mechanics cannot voluntarily expand their prediction window under load any more than a person with impaired lung mechanics can voluntarily increase tidal volume under exertion. The capacity ceiling is physical before it is cognitive.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing for the mechanical ceiling on expansion — pressure mechanics → prediction-window width is established in ANCHOR↔COG. The specific pathway from exhale incompleteness to ambiguity intolerance is mechanistically derived and awaits direct empirical assembly as a testable chain.

---

### Link 2: Pressure failure routes through sympathetic activation

When expansion is attempted and pressure-control capacity is insufficient, the system does not simply fail to expand — it produces a sympathetic spike. The AUTO↔MOD contract describes this substitution: sympathetic load increases precision gain, which actively narrows the prediction window rather than simply failing to widen it. The outcome is a state narrower than the resting baseline, not merely a failure to reach expansion state.

This is also the entry point of the threat-calibration loop formalized in ANCHOR↔COG. The sympathetic spike that fires here is not only the immediate cause of window narrowing — it is the step that, under sustained operation, feeds back into the false ceiling mechanism. Elevated sympathetic tone lowers the CO₂ bracing threshold, which moves bracing onset earlier in the next exhale, which reduces amplitude further, which generates a threat-biased sampling distribution, which recalibrates the prior toward danger, which raises baseline sympathetic tone further. The acute event described in this link is the mechanism by which the prior calibrates into the loop when the event is repeated. Single occurrences produce episodic escalation. Chronic repetition 
produces the prior drift that makes escalation progressively easier to trigger.

This is the mechanistic reason why attempting to engage with an ambiguous or threatening topic under load makes reasoning worse rather than simply less flexible. The attempt itself triggers the sympathetic response that collapses the workspace.

**Chain completeness:** Load-bearing for the sympathetic load → precision gain → window narrowing chain, established in AUTO↔MOD.

### Link 3: Workspace collapse and the bracing substitution

Individuals who lack reliable pressure-control capacity do not typically present as dysregulated at rest. They maintain apparent stability through muscular tension, emotional rigidity, and cognitive narrowing — a bracing pattern that substitutes for the diaphragmatic pressure mechanics the system cannot provide. The brace functions as 
a fake floor: it maintains predictive stability without the oscillatory anchor that genuine regulation requires.

The failure mode specific to this state is the brace collapse event. When the muscular and emotional tension that has been sustaining the floor briefly releases — through fatigue, distraction, or a moment of perceived safety — the diaphragm loses synchrony, sympathetic tone spikes, and the prediction window collapses faster than the gradual deterioration that characterizes ordinary load accumulation. The collapse is sudden and destabilizing. Because the window is already narrow at the moment of collapse, the system cannot distinguish the internal mechanical failure from an external threat. It classifies the collapse as danger arriving from outside.

This is the missing structural link between postural and respiratory mechanics, cognitive workspace collapse, and identity-anchored reasoning. The chain runs through physical structure before it reaches cognitive content.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. The bracing pattern as regulatory substitution is mechanistically coherent with ANCHOR↔COG failure modes. The specific brace-collapse → sympathetic spike → misattributed threat pathway is a framework derivation without direct empirical assembly.

### Link 4: Collapsed workspace and the identity classifier

The COG↔REASON contract establishes that collapsed prediction windows produce high-curvature cognitive manifolds — option spaces compress, counterfactuals are lost, and reasoning shortcuts to the nearest available attractor. The SOC↔COG contract establishes that source identity functions as a precision proxy when content evaluation is unavailable: when the window is too narrow to evaluate the argument, the identity of the source provides a cached, metabolically cheap classification.

The convergence of these two mechanisms is what this link formalizes. When workspace collapse is misattributed as external threat, identity becomes not merely a heuristic but a threat classifier: this topic is dangerous, this source is hostile, this category of person is the problem. The classification is fast, binary, and cached — all properties that metabolic efficiency under threat selects for. It is not ideology being expressed. It is threat classification running on the only classifier available when the workspace has collapsed.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. The convergence of the COG↔REASON curvature mechanism and the SOC↔COG identity-as-precision-proxy mechanism is a cross-contract derivation. The specific identity-classifier activation under brace failure is a framework prediction without direct empirical assembly.

### Link 5: Justification mode as emergency narrative stabilization

Once identity has anchored, the reasoning system no longer functions as an evidence-evaluation mechanism. It functions as a narrative stabilization mechanism. Evidence is filtered by loyalty. Tone becomes a safety signal — the wrong prosodic register confirms threat regardless of content. Emotional coherence takes precedence over factual coherence because emotional coherence is what the system is trying to restore.

This is not a separate failure mode from the fallacy taxonomy in COG↔REASON. It is the same mechanism with the workspace at a more extreme compression point. The COG↔REASON contract describes fallacies as energy-efficient shortcuts in a warped manifold. Justification mode is the state in which the manifold is warped severely enough that every available path leads to the same attractor — the narrative that reduces internal threat. Catastrophizing, goalpost shifting, tone-based classification, binary framing, moralized certainty, and selective evidence are not distinct errors. They are behavioral signatures of a single collapsed state.

The feedback loop identified in COG↔REASON applies here with additional force: expressing justification-mode reasoning under identity anchoring narrows the window further, regardless of whether the social environment challenges or confirms the output. The loop is self-amplifying once identity anchoring is active.

**Chain completeness:** Scaffolding. The justification-mode behavioral signatures are derived mechanistically from COG↔REASON curvature geometry and SOC↔COG identity salience. The self-amplifying feedback loop is a framework derivation. The prediction that precision-state restoration dissolves justification mode independently of argument quality is the primary testable prediction this link generates.

## Failure Modes

**Misattributing collapse as ideological commitment:**
The behavioral signatures of justification mode — binary framing, loyalty filtering, moralized certainty — are phenomenologically identical to strongly held ideological positions. Interventions that target the content (argument, evidence, counter-narrative) address the output while the mechanical substrate remains uncorrected. The workspace is too collapsed to evaluate the intervention, and repeated failure gets attributed to stubbornness or bad faith rather than to the load state that produced the classification in the first place.

**Therapeutic window misidentification:**
Attempting cognitive restructuring, perspective-taking, or values work during active brace-failure or identity-anchored states is contraindicated by the mechanism. The prediction window is too narrow to hold the expansion that these interventions require. The intervention may itself be classified as threat through the identity classifier, producing active resistance. Regulatory stabilization — through pressure mechanics, vagal tone restoration, or external co-regulation — is a prerequisite for the cognitive intervention, not a concurrent component.

**Brace-dependent apparent stability:**
Individuals maintaining stability through bracing present as regulated until the brace fails. Clinical assessment during resting baseline misses the fragility. The diagnostic window is loaded conditions or brief moments of safety — not the resting presentation that reflects successful bracing rather than genuine anchor availability.

## Reactive vs Non-Reactive Profiles — The Learned Association Layer

The escalation pipeline described above assumes a specific trigger condition: 
incoming demand → pressure-control capacity check → failure → sympathetic spike. What varies between individuals is not the pipeline mechanics but the learned association that fires before the pipeline begins.

**The reactive profile** has developed the association: incoming demand → brace first, then engage. The bracing feels like preparation — muscular tension, chest tightening, and held breath present phenomenologically as readiness and seriousness. Every time a difficult situation was navigated while braced, the brace received credit for the outcome. The association deepened: hard things require bracing. The result is that the false ceiling is tightened at the exact moment the incoming demand requires the window to open. The system attributes its own narrowing to the demand rather than to the preparatory response it generated itself.

**The non-reactive profile** has developed the opposite association: incoming demand → open first, then engage. The diaphragm descends, the exhale completes, the bracing threshold rises. The prediction window is wide before the problem arrives. The demand lands into a workspace that can hold multiple interpretations simultaneously rather than one that is already at its false 
ceiling. The non-reactive profile is not more intelligent or more disciplined. The trigger condition for bracing is simply not being met — because the prior weighting around incoming information does not classify ambiguity or complexity as threat. Information arrival reads as something that requires opening, not closing.

The prior weighting difference is the root variable. Bracing as a preparation habit is downstream of a prior that says ambiguity equals potential danger. If the prior does not weight information intake as threatening, the brace reflex does not fire. The non-reactive individual is not overriding the brace through discipline — the trigger condition is absent. This is why telling a 
reactive individual to "just relax" or "stay open" does not produce the non-reactive profile. The instruction targets the output. The prior generating the output has not changed.

The developmental account in INTERO↔DEV applies directly here. The brace habit is historically accurate — for individuals whose early experience produced a threat-biased prior, engaging without bracing repeatedly produced destabilization. The habit locked in before the internal substrate was repaired enough to make the alternative viable. The habit is not irrational. 
It is a learned response to a real regulatory history. The implication is that the prior has to change before the habit can change — which requires sustained wide-window experience that decouples incoming demand from threat signal, not instruction or willpower applied at the habit layer.

## Two Convergent Pathways to the Non-Reactive Profile

The reactive and non-reactive profiles described above both assume the learned association was formed through the same mechanism — repeated social micro-threat exposure during ordinary environmental immersion. The framework generates a more precise prediction: the non-reactive profile can be produced by two mechanistically distinct pathways that converge on identical regulatory outputs.

**Pathway A — Environmental isolation from social micro-threat:**
Sustained absence from environments that deliver the micro-threat conditioning sequence prevents the association from forming. Each repetition of incoming social demand → micro-brace → narrow window → comply is a conditioning event. Without the repetitions, the association does not write. The nervous system never learns that incoming social demand equals potential danger. Information arrival continues to read as information rather than as threat. The prediction window never learns to collapse in response to hierarchy, evaluation, or ambiguity. Eight years of remote work is the environmental version of this pathway — not just fitting a personality, but actively preventing a conditioning sequence that would have written a different prior.

**Pathway B — Reduced social cue detection:**
Some individuals do not pick up the micro-expressions, tone shifts, prosodic hierarchical signals, and implicit status cues that carry the micro-threat information. The conditioning events arrive but the signal that would trigger the micro-brace — the cue that encodes "this is an evaluation moment, this is a status signal, this is a hierarchy signal requiring compliance" — is not received at sufficient precision to generate the conditioned response. The result is structurally identical to Pathway A: the threat-coupling never forms because the signal that would have driven it was not consistently detected.

This is the mechanistic account of why some ND individuals appear "fearless" in hierarchical contexts — not because they are brave or defiant, but because the signal that normally teaches the hierarchical brace reflex did not register at the precision required to write the association. The behavior that follows — questioning rules, challenging hierarchy, responding to logical content rather than social rank — is not rebellion. It is the genuine content-evaluation response operating without the social threat filter that was never installed.

The practical expression is precise: a person without the hierarchical threat-coupling will respond to what was said rather than to who said it or what social consequence responding carries. They are listening to the narrative and the logic of the situation. They have not associated those evaluation cues with the brace response that would redirect their output through the hierarchy 
filter. This reads as socially inappropriate to people whose threat-coupling is fully formed — because from inside a conditioned system, bypassing the hierarchy filter looks like either obliviousness or defiance. It is neither. It is the absence of a learned interception layer.

**Why the two pathways produce identical outputs:**
Both pathways produce the same downstream state: incoming social demand does not trigger the brace reflex, the prediction window does not collapse in response to hierarchy or evaluation, and the reasoning system responds to content rather than to social threat classification. The phenomenology differs — the environmentally isolated individual may be able to recall learning the uncollapsed state; the reduced-cue-detection individual may have no memory of it being otherwise. The regulatory output is the same. The prior that generates it was written through different routes to the same destination.

The social mismatch consequence is also identical. In both cases, the person is operating with a prior that is a minority prior in a population where social threat-coupling is the statistical baseline. Their responses will be read as inappropriate, naive, or provocative by individuals whose compliance filter intercepts the same content before it reaches speech. The misread is not 
about the content of what was said — it is about the absence of the filter the majority assumes is running.

## Predictions This Contract Generates

**Prediction 1 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Individuals with documented low exhale completeness (measured as reduced E:I ratio under load) should show lower ambiguity-tolerance thresholds than individuals with equivalent resting HRV but higher exhale completeness — establishing pressure-control capacity as an independent predictor of ambiguity tolerance, separable from general autonomic regulation.

**Prediction 2 — Chain completeness: Scaffolding**
Identity-relevance manipulations should produce measurably larger prediction-window compression in individuals with degraded pressure-control capacity than in individuals with intact pressure mechanics — confirming that identity-classifier activation is downstream of mechanical failure rather than of belief strength or personality variables.

**Prediction 3 — Chain completeness: Predictive**
Interventions that restore pressure mechanics (slow exhalation, diaphragmatic training, postural correction) should reduce justification-mode reasoning frequency and duration in high-conflict social contexts — independently of the cognitive content being discussed and without direct engagement with the identity-anchored beliefs. Effect size should exceed that of argument-quality interventions in matched populations, confirming the upstream mechanical mechanism.

**Prediction 4 — Chain completeness: Predictive**
Brace-collapse events should be detectable as HRV discontinuities preceding the onset of identity-anchored language in naturalistic conversation data — establishing the temporal sequence from mechanical failure to cognitive output and distinguishing the collapse mechanism from gradual load accumulation.

## Origin Note

This contract emerged from a casual observation about the difference between people who can sit with ambiguity and people who immediately reach for an identity classifier when a topic feels threatening. The existing contracts each described part of the mechanism — ANCHOR↔COG had the pressure-window link, AUTO↔MOD had the sympathetic spike, SOC↔COG had the identity classifier, COG↔REASON had the curvature geometry — but the escalation pathway connecting them in sequence had no formal location.

The missing piece was the bracing substitution: the observation that people without genuine pressure-control capacity maintain apparent stability through muscular and emotional bracing, and that brace failure produces a collapse faster and more total than ordinary load accumulation. That collapse is the actual trigger for identity anchoring — not the content of the triggering topic but the mechanical failure that the topic's ambiguity demand exposed.

The implication for intervention design is the most actionable output: address the floor before addressing the content. Regulatory stabilization is a prerequisite for the cognitive work, not its concurrent component.

*Chain status: Links 1–2 are load-bearing from upstream contracts. Links 3–5 are scaffolding — mechanistically coherent derivations from cross-contract convergence without direct empirical assembly. Prediction 3 is the primary testable output: pressure-mechanics intervention should reduce justification-mode frequency independently of cognitive content engagement.*
